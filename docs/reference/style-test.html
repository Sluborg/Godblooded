Tier 1: children of Thor, Odin, Loki and Freyja.

Godblooded Style Test

A cartoon, RimWorld-like look drawn entirely in code, with a painterly finish: soft light from the top left, rim light, brush texture and metal glints. Every hero and monster is built from parts (feet, body, head, headgear, cape, hands, weapon) and every motion is code: walk, breathe, swing, cast, flinch, fall, cheer.

The loop: the party of Eirik leaves town for the bounty flag, draugr pour out of the barrow, Narfi is knocked out and Astrid revives him, the party levels up and walks home.

Heroes: Eirik (Thor, hammer and blue sparks), Grim (Odin, wide hat and raven), Narfi (Loki, twin daggers and green flame), Astrid (Freyja, feather cloak and gold healing glow).

Growing up: a path never swaps the hero. Tier 2 keeps the face, hair, skin, palette and silhouette and adds gear and effects; tier 3 is the same path with more of it: bigger, brighter gear, a longer cape, a small aura and one extra ornament.

Read at a glance: blue rings and names mark the party; red rings and dark, cold colours mark monsters. Numbers take the damage type colour: physical white, lightning yellow, holy gold, death green, fire orange, frost blue.
