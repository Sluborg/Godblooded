Skeleton test: the axeman

A real skeleton with bones, joints that bend themselves, and timed poses. Head, hands and feet are art; the rest are plain shapes until rig-ready art exists.

game size

How it works: hips carry the torso and head; each shoulder carries an upper arm (the sleeve), a forearm and a fist; each hip carries a thigh, a shin and a boot. The game only places the feet, the hands and the hips; knees and elbows bend by themselves to reach. Walk, attack and hit are a handful of timed poses with smooth curves between them. Tap Bones to see the skeleton.
