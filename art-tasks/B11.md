## Ground tiles (items 1-5): seamless textures, no background colour

- The **whole canvas is ground**, seen straight from above, edge to edge. **No key colour, no
  border, no frame, no objects** (no rocks or flowers bigger than a few pixels).
- **Plain grass with tufts**: a calm, even field with small scattered
  grass tufts like the reference, nothing busy.
- **Seamless:** the game repeats the tile in a grid, so the left edge must continue into the
  right edge and the top into the bottom with no visible seam. Keep the pattern even: no light
  or dark corner, no vignette, no single feature that would repeat as a visible stamp.
- Soft painterly ground in calm, slightly muted colours (like the green of the shipped buildings' surroundings) so units
  and buildings stand out on top. No outline on the ground.

1. `ter_ground_grass_a`: plain warm olive-green grass with small scattered tufts and a few
   slightly darker patches.
2. `ter_ground_grass_b`: the same grass as item 1 with a few tiny dry yellow-brown specks and
   very small white flowers (a variant tile that mixes with item 1).
3. `ter_ground_grass_c`: the same grass, a little worn: thin patches of bare earth showing
   through (to mix near the town).
4. `ter_ground_dirt`: packed brown earth, small pebbles, faint wheel ruts going nowhere in
   particular (for the town square and roads).
5. `ter_ground_scorched`: burnt ground: black and dark grey ash, a few glowing orange cracks,
   dead stubble (around the wyrm nest and burnt-out places).
