# B3: heroes tier 1, paladin and rogue (4 images)

Track: heroes. Art request 10.

Follow `STYLE.md` (classic fantasy strategy, about 4.5 heads tall, weathered, painted) and
`docs/asset-spec.md` (framing, views). Match the look of the reference warrior
`art-tasks/ref/hero_warrior_t1_front.png` (fetch it raw): same proportions, outline, shading and
camera. Square canvas at your normal output size, one item per image, nothing else in it.

Units: front = faces the camera, turned 3/4 toward the lower right; back = **the same
character** from behind, turned 3/4 toward the upper right. Whole figure visible, feet at about
88% of the canvas height, figure about 75% of the canvas height, centred, feet planted in a ready
stance. Tier 1 = fresh recruit: worn leather and iron, muted earthy colours, plus the class colour
on a big area.

Each unit's **strike** sets where its weapon is drawn (table in `STYLE.md`). This draft is
rewritten for the chunky look and the face-right pose standard before it goes Ready.

1. `hero_paladin_t1_front` (**strike: smash**): a young Godblood paladin, the sturdiest hero. **Class colour:
   crimson** tabard over chain mail with a plain sun disc on the chest. Open iron helmet, a big
   iron-rimmed kite shield on the left arm, a warhammer gripped at the lower end of the handle,
   head forward. Broad, calm, determined. Flat green #00FF00 background.
2. `hero_paladin_t1_back` (**strike: smash**): the same paladin from behind; tabard, shield and hammer read clearly.
   Flat green #00FF00 background.
3. `hero_rogue_t1_front` (**strike: double chop**): a young Godblood rogue, the smallest and quickest hero. **Class colour:
   burnt orange** hood and short cape. Dark fitted leather with many pouches, a scarf over the
   mouth, a curved dagger in each hand held point forward. Light crouching stance. Flat green
   #00FF00 background.
4. `hero_rogue_t1_back` (**strike: double chop**): the same rogue from behind; hood, pouches and both daggers read clearly.
   Flat green #00FF00 background.
