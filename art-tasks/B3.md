# B3: heroes tier 1, paladin and rogue (4 images)

Track: heroes. Art request 10.

Follow `STYLE.md` (chunky pixel art, faces right, weapon placed by the strike) and
`docs/asset-spec.md`. **Look reference: `art-tasks/ref/chunky-warrior.png`** (fetch it raw): match
its proportions (about 3.5 to 4 heads, big head, big hands and feet, oversized weapon), thick dark
outline, pixel shading and camera. More chunky references in `art-tasks/ref/` (`chunky-*.png`),
concept sheets in `docs/concept/`. Square canvas at your normal output size, one item per image,
nothing else in it.

Every unit is **one still picture per view**, no frames: the game does all motion in code.

- **front:** faces RIGHT, a three-quarter view toward the lower right.
- **back:** **the same character** from behind, turned three-quarter toward the upper right; the
  weapon in the same strike position, seen from behind.
- Whole figure visible, feet at about 88% of the canvas height, figure about 75% of the canvas
  height, centred, wide planted stance. Nothing cut off, the whole weapon inside the picture.
- **Strike** (table in `STYLE.md`): it fixes where the weapon is drawn, so the game's strike
  starts there. Each item names it and spells out the pose.

Tier 1 = fresh recruit: worn leather and iron, scuffed and dark, no glow; the class colour on a
big area so the class reads at phone size.

1. `hero_paladin_t1_front` (**strike: smash**): a young Godblood paladin, the sturdiest hero.
   **Class colour: crimson** tabard over chain mail with a plain sun disc on the chest. Open iron
   helmet, a big iron-rimmed kite shield on the front arm. **Pose:** an oversized warhammer
   **raised over the head** in the back hand, gripped at the lower end of the handle, ready to
   smash down. Broad, calm, determined. Flat green #00FF00 background.
2. `hero_paladin_t1_back` (**strike: smash**): the same paladin from behind, the hammer raised over
   the head; tabard, shield and hammer read clearly. Flat green #00FF00 background.
3. `hero_rogue_t1_front` (**strike: double chop**): a young Godblood rogue, the smallest and
   quickest hero. **Class colour: burnt orange** hood and short cape. Dark fitted leather with
   many pouches, a scarf over the mouth. **Pose** (like `art-tasks/ref/chunky-duelist.png`): a
   curved dagger in each hand, **both clearly visible**: one raised high behind the back
   shoulder, the other held low in front of the body. Light crouching stance. **Flat magenta #FF00FF background.**
4. `hero_rogue_t1_back` (**strike: double chop**): the same rogue from behind, both daggers in the
   same places; hood, pouches and both daggers read clearly. **Flat magenta #FF00FF background.**
