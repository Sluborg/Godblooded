# A2: the eight Aesir paths at tier 3 (8 images)

Track: heroes. Art request 210 (Lead 2026-10-13, M2). Design: `docs/game-design.md` "Gods and
paths". Tier 3 is the last upgrade: the hero becomes a near-god of the path.

Follow `STYLE.md` (chunky pixel art, faces right, weapon placed by the strike) and
`docs/asset-spec.md`. **Look reference: `art-tasks/ref/chunky-warrior.png`** (fetch it raw).
**Each item is the upgrade of its shipped tier-2 picture**: fetch it raw from
`public/assets/units/hero_<god>-<path>_t2_front.png`, keep face, hair, build, colours and the
**same pose and weapon position** (the strike starts there). Square canvas at your normal output
size, one item per image, nothing else in it.

- **front:** faces RIGHT, a three-quarter view toward the lower right.
- Whole figure visible, feet at about 88% of the canvas height, figure about 75% of the canvas
  height (the same size as tier 2), centred, wide planted stance. Nothing cut off.
- **Tier 3 = divine:** gold trim, finer armour, one glowing motif of the god, a **bigger
  silhouette from the outfit** (a crest, a tall helm, a wide mantle, folded wings, a bigger
  weapon head), not from a bigger body. A soft glow is allowed **close to the body and weapon**;
  no wide aura, no rays, no spread wings, nothing reaching the canvas edge.

1. `hero_odin-seer_t3_front` (**strike: bolt**): **Seer** made all-seeing: a deep navy and gold
   cloak with a mantle of raven feathers, wide-brimmed hat with a gold band, gold eye-patch,
   glowing blue runes circling the staff head, **two ravens**, one on each shoulder. **Pose:**
   the spear-staff in the **FRONT hand**, its spearhead raised at shoulder height in front of the
   body; a bright rune in the free hand. **Flat magenta #FF00FF background.**
2. `hero_odin-wanderer_t3_front` (**strike: thrust**): **Wanderer** become Odin's spear: a long
   grey and gold cloak, a gold-trimmed hat, gold eye-patch, gilded mail, a wolf-fur mantle, the
   long spear now **Gungnir-like** with a broad gold-etched head and glowing blue runes. **Pose:**
   the spear in both hands **level at chest height, tip pointing forward** (to the right).
   **Flat magenta #FF00FF background.**
3. `hero_thor-hammerborn_t3_front` (**strike: smash**): **Hammerborn** become Thor's champion:
   heavy gilded plate, a winged helm with the wings folded back, a red cape, lightning crackling
   along the arms, a **Mjolnir-like** hammer with a big carved head and gold bands. **Pose:** the
   hammer **raised over the head** in both hands. **Flat magenta #FF00FF background.**
4. `hero_thor-stormcaller_t3_front` (**strike: bolt**): **Stormcaller** become the storm: storm

   hand** at shoulder height in front of the body, a bright lightning crackle at its tip; a

5. `hero_loki-trickster_t3_front` (**strike: double chop**): **Trickster** become shapeshifter: a

6. `hero_loki-flamebinder_t3_front` (**strike: bolt**): **Flamebinder** become wildfire: charred

7. `hero_freyja-valkyrie_t3_front` (**strike: overhead chop**): **Valkyrie** become chooser of

8. `hero_freyja-seidkona_t3_front` (**strike: bolt**): **Seidkona** become Freyja's voice: a
