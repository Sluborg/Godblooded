# A2: the eight Aesir paths at tier 3, as parts sheets (8 images)

Track: heroes. Art request 210 (M2). **Run after H2 is shipped**: each item is the tier-2 sheet
(`public/assets/units/hero_<god>-<path>_t2_parts.png`, fetch raw) with the tier-3 additions;
everything else stays.

Follow `STYLE.md` (Stefan's taste test: **big-head cartoon, thin colour-matched outlines,
painterly shading, warm earthy muted palette**) and `docs/asset-spec.md` **"Parts sheets"**.
Look reference: fetch raw `docs/reference/style-test-1.png` for proportions; follow `STYLE.md`,
not its thick black outlines. Looks come from Design's table, `docs/systems/paths-and-skills.md`
section 6 ("How each path looks").

**Each item is one parts sheet:** a square image split into a **5 x 2 grid**, one part centred in
each cell, nothing crossing into another cell, **flat magenta #FF00FF everywhere else**, all parts
at **the same scale**, facing right. Cells, left to right, top row first: **body** (torso with
clothing) · **head** (face; a beard belongs to the head) · **headgear** (hair, hood or helmet) ·
**cape** · **weapon** (alone, by its grip) / **front foot** · **back foot** · **front hand** ·
**back hand** · **shield** (empty if none). **No effects** in any part (no sparks, flames, glow,
ravens, clouds, shimmer): the game adds them. A path keeps its god child's face, hair and
palette (`docs/asset-spec.md` Tiers).

1. `hero_odin-seer_t3_parts`: a gold-banded hat, a raven-feather mantle (cape).
2. `hero_odin-wanderer_t3_parts`: a Gungnir-style gold spearhead, a wolf-pelt mantle.
3. `hero_thor-hammerborn_t3_parts`: gilded plate, the helm gains wings folded back.
4. `hero_thor-stormcaller_t3_parts`: finer storm-blue robes with gold trim (the storm cloud and
   arcs are added by the game).
5. `hero_loki-trickster_t3_parts`: a finer purple hood with gold trim, gilded daggers (the
   mirror shimmer is added by the game).
6. `hero_loki-flamebinder_t3_parts`: a charred robe with gold trim, the hood shaped with two
   horn points (the game sets them alight).
7. `hero_freyja-valkyrie_t3_parts`: a gold-trimmed helm with wings folded close, a white fur
   mantle, a feathered blue-violet cloak.
8. `hero_freyja-seidkona_t3_parts`: a frosted cape hem, finer frost robes (the ring of ice
   shards is added by the game).
