# S1: art direction, five styles of one warrior (5 images)

Track: heroes. **Priority: make this before anything else.** Stefan picks the game's art
direction from these five. Same character in all five, only the style changes, so the choice
is about the look.

**The character (all five):** a young Norse Godblood warrior, tier 1 (fresh recruit): simple
round iron helmet with a nose guard, brown braids, worn brown leather jerkin over a blue tunic,
leather boots, a round wooden shield with an iron rim on the left arm, an iron axe in the right
hand. Reference for the character design: `art-tasks/ref/hero_warrior_t1_front.png`.

**The grip (all five, important):** hold the axe like a real fighter: the right hand grips the
**lower end of the handle**, the axe head points **forward and up**, ready to strike. The handle
does not rest against the arm or shoulder. Feet planted apart in a ready stance.

**Framing (all five):** 3/4 front view (turned toward the lower right), whole figure visible,
feet at about 88% of the canvas height, figure about 75% of the canvas height, centred. Flat
green #00FF00 background, nothing else in the picture. Square canvas at your normal size.

1. `hero_warrior-a_t1_front`: **baseline**, the reference look: chibi, about 2.5 heads tall,
   painterly cel shading, dark-brown outline. Redraw the reference with the new grip.
2. `hero_warrior-b_t1_front`: **flat mobile strategy**: flat cel colours with almost no
   gradients, bold thick dark outlines, few colours, very clean shapes, about 3 heads tall; reads
   instantly at small size (like Kingdom Rush or Clash of Clans units).
3. `hero_warrior-c_t1_front`: **classic fantasy strategy**: more realistic proportions, about
   4.5 heads tall, less cute, slightly grim and weathered, painted shading (like the units of the
   old PC game Majesty).
4. `hero_warrior-d_t1_front`: **storybook**: hand-drawn ink line with watercolour fills, slightly
   uneven line, muted Nordic palette (slate blue, moss, rust, bone), about 3 heads tall.
5. `hero_warrior-e_t1_front`: **chunky pixel art**: large crisp square pixels (the figure about
   64 pixels tall, scaled up with hard edges), limited palette of about 16 colours, no
   anti-aliasing, big readable shapes.

Deliver as usual (`S1--<id>.png`). Report anything that looked off.
