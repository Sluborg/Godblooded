## Panel and buttons (stretched by the game)

The game stretches the panel and the buttons to any size with **9-slice**: the four corners stay
as drawn, the edges stretch along their length, the middle stretches both ways. So:

- All ornament (rivets, corner caps, studs) sits **in the corners only**, each corner inside the
  outer **15%** of the shape's width and height.
- The four edges between the corners are a **plain, even band** (same thickness, no ornament, no
  gradient along its length) so they stretch without smearing.
- The middle is a **flat, even fill** (a faint grain is fine, no pattern, no picture).

1. `ui_panel`: a dialog panel: a thick dark iron frame with bronze corner caps and rivets, the
   middle a flat deep navy-black (worn leather look). The panel fills about **94% of the canvas**
   both ways, centred, square corners slightly bevelled.
2. `ui_button`: a button in its normal state: a wide bar about **90% of the canvas width and 32%
   of its height**, centred: a bronze rim with a dark outline, small iron studs in the four
   corners, the middle a flat warm bronze-brown fill, a light top edge (raised).
3. `ui_button_pressed`: **an edit of item 2**, same canvas, size and position: the same button
   pushed in: the middle a shade darker, the light edge moved to the bottom (sunken), the studs
   unchanged.
4. `ui_button_disabled`: **an edit of item 2**, same canvas, size and position: the same button
   greyed out: dull grey iron rim and studs, a flat dark grey middle, no gold, no highlight.

colours, no fine detail. Bronze-gold and iron, plus one accent colour where named.
