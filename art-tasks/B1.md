# B1: style test (4 images)

The first images of the game. They set the look every later batch copies, so Stefan approves
them before any other batch is made. **Track: heroes**; this one batch also holds a monster and a
building so the three kinds are judged side by side.

Follow `STYLE.md` and `docs/asset-spec.md` (framing, views). Square canvas at your normal output
size, one item per image, nothing else in it.

1. `hero_warrior_t1_front`: a young Godblood warrior, tier 1 (fresh recruit). Chibi, about 2.5
   heads tall, brave grin. Simple round iron helmet with a nose guard, short brown braids, worn
   brown leather jerkin over a dull blue tunic, leather bracers and boots. An oversized iron axe
   in the right hand, a round wooden shield with iron rim on the left arm. Muted colours, few big
   shapes. Front view: faces the camera, turned 3/4 toward the lower right. Whole figure visible,
   feet at about 88% of the canvas height, figure about 75% of the canvas height, centred.
   Flat green #00FF00 background.
2. `hero_warrior_t1_back`: **the same warrior** as item 1 (same helmet, braids, clothes, axe,
   shield, proportions and colours), seen from behind: faces away, turned 3/4 toward the upper
   right. The shield shows on the back of the left arm, the axe over the right shoulder. Same
   framing as item 1. Flat green #00FF00 background.
3. `mon_draugr_t1_front`: a draugr, an undead Norse warrior risen from a burial mound, tier 1.
   Chibi like the hero but hunched and gaunt, grey-green skin, two small pale blue eyes, a rusty
   dented helmet, a torn dark cloak, a notched old sword. Spooky but cute, not gory. Front view,
   same framing as item 1. **Flat magenta #FF00FF background** (the skin is green).
4. `bld_townhall_t1`: the town hall, tier 1: a small Norse wooden longhouse with a steep turf
   roof, carved dragon heads on both gable ends, a big double door with iron bands and a red
   banner by the door. 3/4 top-down game camera, seen from the lower right. Whole building
   visible, its base centred, about 85% of the canvas width. No ground around it, no path.
   **Flat magenta #FF00FF background** (the turf roof is green).

Report anything that looked off (hands, extra objects, background not flat) when you deliver.
