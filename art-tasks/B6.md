# B6: buildings tier 1, the three temples (3 images)

Track: buildings. Art request 30. Each temple calls Godbloods of its pantheon, so the three must
read apart at a glance by shape and colour.

Follow `STYLE.md` (chunky pixel art: crisp pixels, thick dark outline, gritty palette) and
`docs/asset-spec.md`. Look reference for the rendering: `art-tasks/ref/chunky-warrior.png` (fetch
it raw): same outline weight, pixel shading and light from the top left. Square canvas at your
normal output size, one item per image, nothing else in it.

- 3/4 top-down game camera seen from the lower right, the same camera for every building.
- Whole building visible, its base centred, about 85% of the canvas width. Chunky and a little
  oversized in its details (doors, beams, roof ornaments) so it reads at phone size.
- No ground, grass, path or scenery around it, no shadow.
- Tier 1 = humble and weathered: dark wood, rough stone, worn iron, simple shapes.

1. `bld_temple_aesir_t1`: Aesir temple: a small Norse stave temple, dark tarred wood, two stacked
   steep shingle roofs with carved dragon heads, a round rune stone by the door, frost-blue and
   silver painted trim. **Flat magenta #FF00FF background.**
2. `bld_temple_greek_t1`: Greek temple: a small weathered marble temple with four columns, a
   triangular roof with a plain pediment, two steps, a bronze brazier at the entrance,
   bronze-gold trim. **Flat magenta #FF00FF background.**
3. `bld_temple_egyptian_t1`: Egyptian temple: a small sandstone pylon gate with sloped walls, a
   doorway between, two short obelisks in front, faded lapis-blue and gold painted bands. **Flat magenta #FF00FF background.**
