# B6: buildings tier 1, the three temples (3 images)

Track: buildings. Art request 30. Each temple calls Godbloods of its pantheon, so the three must
read apart at a glance by shape and colour.

Follow `STYLE.md` (classic fantasy strategy, about 4.5 heads tall, weathered, painted) and
`docs/asset-spec.md` (framing, views). Match the look of the reference warrior
`art-tasks/ref/hero_warrior_t1_front.png` (fetch it raw): same proportions, outline, shading and
camera. Square canvas at your normal output size, one item per image, nothing else in it.

Buildings: 3/4 top-down game camera seen from the lower right, the same camera for every
building. Whole building visible, its base centred, about 85% of the canvas width. No ground,
grass, path or scenery around it. Tier 1 = humble and weathered: wood, rough stone, simple
shapes, painted like the reference warrior.

1. `bld_temple_aesir_t1`: Aesir temple: a small Norse stave temple, dark tarred wood, two stacked
   steep shingle roofs with carved dragon heads, a round rune stone by the door, frost-blue and
   silver painted trim. Flat green #00FF00 background.
2. `bld_temple_greek_t1`: Greek temple: a small weathered marble temple with four columns, a
   triangular roof with a plain pediment, two steps, a bronze brazier at the entrance,
   laurel-gold trim. Flat green #00FF00 background.
3. `bld_temple_egyptian_t1`: Egyptian temple: a small sandstone pylon gate with sloped walls, a
   doorway between, two short obelisks in front, faded lapis-blue and gold painted bands. Flat
   green #00FF00 background.
