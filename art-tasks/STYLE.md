# Godblooded house style (every image, every chat)

Apply to every image on top of the batch text, so all chats make art that fits together.
Stefan's own concept sheets are the reference: `docs/concept/` (README there). Heroes:
`docs/concept/heroes-sheet-*.jpg`; monsters: `docs/concept/monsters-*.jpg`.

- **Look (heroes):** detailed **hi-bit pixel art**: crisp square pixels, no blur, no painterly
  soft edges, no anti-aliasing against the background. Grounded, near-realistic proportions (about 6 heads).
  Gritty and dark: worn leather, scratched bronze, torn cloth, serious faces.
- **Look (monsters):** grim and dangerous, same pixel-art rendering as the heroes so they share
  a screen; designs follow the painted concept bestiaries.
- **Palette:** dark base of black, deep navy and bronze-gold, plus leather browns. Each Godblood
  keeps one signature accent from its divine parent (Thor: storm blue-white, Bastet: moon
  violet, ...). Tier 1 has no glow; magic glow and effects grow with the tier.
- **Lines:** a strong dark outline around the figure.
- **Shading:** pixel shading with light from the top left; no cast shadow, no shadow ellipse
  under the feet, no glow outside the object unless the batch asks for a tier-3 aura.
- **Animation-ready:** simple, clear shapes that survive frame-by-frame edits; weapons held the
  way a real fighter holds them (axe and hammer gripped at the lower end of the handle).
- **Phone readability:** units show about 60-80 px tall; the silhouette, weapon and accent
  colour carry the read.
- **Camera:** the same front view turned slightly toward the lower right for every unit; 3/4
  top-down for buildings.
- **Background:** flat magenta #FF00FF (the concept sheets use it; the palette has little
  magenta). Use flat green #00FF00 only when the batch says so.
- No text, no logos, no watermark, no frame, no background scenery: only the object on the flat
  key colour.
