# Godblooded house style (every image, every chat)

Apply to every image on top of the batch text, so all chats make art that fits together.

- **Look:** chibi fantasy. Big heads (about 2.5 heads tall), short sturdy bodies, oversized
  weapons and helmets. Cute but brave, never babyish. Bold, readable silhouettes.
- **World:** an alternate medieval world where the old gods never left. Norse (Aesir), Greek
  and Egyptian flavours mix. Mythic monsters.
- **Lines:** clean dark-brown outline, medium weight, even all around.
- **Shading:** cel shading, two tones plus one soft highlight, light from the top left. No cast
  shadows, no drop shadows, no glow outside the object.
- **Palette:** warm and saturated. Pantheon accents: Aesir frost blue and silver, Greek white,
  laurel gold and bronze, Egyptian lapis blue and gold. Monsters lean darker and greener.
- **Detail:** readable at 64 px tall on a phone. Few big shapes, no texture noise, no tiny text.
- **Camera:** the same 3/4 top-down game camera for every unit and building.
- No text, no logos, no watermark, no frame, no background scenery: only the object on the flat
  key colour.
