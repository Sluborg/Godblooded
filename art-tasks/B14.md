# B14: buildings tier 3, all eight (8 images)

Track: buildings. Art request 200 (Lead 2026-10-13, M2). The player upgrades a building a second
time (tier 3, "divine"); the picture replaces the tier-2 one on the same spot.

Follow `STYLE.md` (chunky pixel art: crisp pixels, thick dark outline, gritty palette) and
`docs/asset-spec.md`. Look reference for the rendering: `art-tasks/ref/chunky-warrior.png` (fetch
it raw): same outline weight, pixel shading and light from the top left. **Each item is the
upgrade of its shipped tier-2 picture**: fetch it raw from
`public/assets/buildings/bld_<type>_t2.png` and keep its layout, main shape and colours
recognisable. Square canvas at your normal output size, one item per image, nothing else in it.

- 3/4 top-down game camera seen from the lower right, the same camera as tier 1 and 2.
- Whole building visible, its base centred, about **92% of the canvas width** (a little larger
  than tier 2), with a clear margin on every side so nothing touches the edge.
- No ground, grass, path or scenery around it, no shadow.
- **Tier 3 = divine:** the tier-2 building blessed by the gods. Gold trim and gilded roof
  ornaments, one **glowing motif of the pantheon** (soft glow inside the building's outline, no
  rays or particles outside it), a taller or grander silhouette: a spire, a dome, a great gate or
  a second tower. The silhouette must change from tier 2, not only the colours.
- Norse buildings (items 1-6) glow frost-blue runes; the Greek temple glows warm gold; the
  Egyptian temple glows lapis blue and gold.

1. `bld_townhall_t3`: the jarl's hall become a king's hall: a tall central tower with a gilded
   dragon on top, gold-capped carved gables, glowing blue runes over the great doors, four
   banners. **Flat magenta #FF00FF background.**
2. `bld_market_t3`: a grand market hall: a raised timber hall with a gilded weather vane, a
   balcony over the stalls, chests of gold coins and fine goods, a large carved signboard with a
   glowing rune. **Flat magenta #FF00FF background.**
3. `bld_smithy_t3`: a dwarf-worthy forge: a stone forge-house with a tall chimney, a giant anvil
   with glowing blue runes, a rack of gilded weapons and shields, a forge fire glowing blue-white.
   **Flat magenta #FF00FF background.**
4. `bld_shrine_t3`: a holy sanctuary: the round shrine grown into a small domed temple with a gold
   rim, a glowing spring in a wide basin, white linen banners with gold edges, a ring of candles.
   **Flat magenta #FF00FF background.**
5. `bld_tower_t3`: a great watchtower: tall stone tower with two lookout levels, a gilded spire
   roof, a large mounted ballista, a glowing blue rune stone set in the wall, a red and gold
   banner. **Flat magenta #FF00FF background.**
6. `bld_temple_aesir_t3`: a hall of the gods: the stave church grown to four stacked roofs, gilded
   dragon heads, a great carved portal with glowing frost-blue runes, two tall rune stones with
   gold inlay, a gilded world-tree emblem on the front. **Flat magenta #FF00FF background.**
7. `bld_temple_greek_t3`: an Olympian temple: eight marble columns, a gilded pediment with a
   glowing gold relief, a gold statue on the roof ridge, two tall bronze braziers with gold fire.
   **Flat magenta #FF00FF background.**
8. `bld_temple_egyptian_t3`: a great sun temple: tall pylons with gilded tops, two gold-capped
   obelisks, a winged sun disc over the door glowing lapis and gold, sphinx statues with gold
   headdresses. **Flat magenta #FF00FF background.**
