# U4: item icons (11 images)

Track: ui. Art request 130 (backlog Art 140). Do this **after U2** and draw in exactly the U2 icon
style (`art-tasks/U2.md`, "Icons"): when U2 icons are shipped, fetch two raw from
`public/assets/ui/` (for example `ui_icon_gold.png`, `ui_icon_upgrade.png`) and match them.

Follow `STYLE.md` (chunky pixel art, thick dark outline, gritty palette). Square canvas at your
normal output size, one item per image, **flat magenta #FF00FF background**, no text.

Each icon is a **single bold object** filling about **80% of the canvas**, centred, no plate or
frame behind it. It must **read at 32 px**: big shapes, two or three colours, no fine detail.
Shown in the hero panel (the four gear slots) and over shops when a hero buys.

1. `ui_item_sword`: a broad iron sword, point up, bronze cross-guard (weapon slot).
2. `ui_item_focus`: a short carved wooden staff head with a glowing blue rune stone (caster weapon).
3. `ui_item_armour`: an iron mail shirt with bronze trim (armour slot).
4. `ui_item_potion`: a small round glass flask of red potion with a cork.
5. `ui_item_potion_greater`: a bigger flask of deep red potion with a gold band and a small
   sparkle (clearly the stronger one next to item 4).
6. `ui_item_charm_hawkeye`: a hawk's eye amulet: a gold disc with an amber eye.
7. `ui_item_charm_foxtail`: a red fox tail tied with a leather cord.
8. `ui_item_charm_emberward`: a round amulet split in half, one half flame orange, one half
   frost blue.
9. `ui_item_charm_graveward`: a small bone talisman with a silver rune, tied with black cord.
10. `ui_item_charm_bloodstone`: a dark red gemstone in an iron setting with a drop shape inside.
11. `ui_item_charm_wardring`: a heavy bronze ring with a small shield rune on it.
