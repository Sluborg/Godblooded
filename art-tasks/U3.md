# U3: splash key art (1 image)

Track: ui. Art request 100 (backlog Art 110). Do this **after U2**. The title screen shows this
painting full screen with the logo laid on top in code, so it has **no text and no logo**.

Follow `STYLE.md` for the look (chunky pixel art, crisp pixels, thick dark outlines, gritty
palette) and match the heroes and monsters already in the game: fetch raw
`art-tasks/ref/chunky-warrior.png`, `art-tasks/ref/chunky-draugr.png`,
`art-tasks/ref/chunky-troll.png` and `art-tasks/ref/concept-thor-t1-t2.png`. This is the one
picture that is a **full scene**: no key colour, the painting fills the whole canvas edge to edge.

**Canvas and framing.** The game screen is wide (16:9).

- If you can draw a **landscape** canvas, use it (3:2, for example 1536 x 1024). Keep everything
  important between **8% and 92% of the height**: the game crops a thin strip of sky at the top
  and ground at the bottom.
- If you can only draw a **square**, paint the scene across the full width and keep everything
  important between **22% and 78% of the height** (the middle 16:9 strip). Above it is only sky,
  below it only ground: the game crops both.
- Leave the **upper middle calm** (sky, clouds, a low sun): the logo goes there.

1. `art_splash`: **Godbloods at dusk.** In the lower left third, a small band of five Godbloods
   seen from behind and three-quarter, facing right toward the enemy: a Daughter of Thor with a
   raised axe (storm-blue), a Son of Odin with a staff and a raven on his shoulder (navy), a Greek
   hoplite with a round bronze shield and spear (red crest), an Egyptian archer with a bow
   (white linen, gold collar), a Son of Loki with two daggers (dark green). In the right half, a
   **monster horde** coming over a ridge: draugr with rusty axes, a troll, a minotaur, a
   jackal-man, harpies in the sky, and far behind them the silhouette of a great wyrm. Between
   them a stretch of trampled ground. Behind the heroes, at the far left edge, a small Norse
   town with a stave temple and warm lit windows. Sky: **dusk**, a low orange-red sun behind the
   horde, deep navy above, embers in the air. Dramatic, readable shapes, strong silhouettes
   against the sky. **No text, no letters, no logo, no frame.**
