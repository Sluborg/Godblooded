Square canvas at your normal output size, one figure per image, nothing else in it, **flat

Town folk are ordinary Norse townsfolk, smaller and plainer than the heroes, friendly-stern.

1. `npc_collector_t1_front`: **the reeve** who collects the town's tax. A stocky middle-aged man
   with a trimmed grey beard, a warm brown wool tunic, a short fur-collared cloak, a belt of keys.
   A **thick leather-bound ledger** under the back arm and a **small iron-bound coin chest** held
   in the front hand, a full coin pouch at the belt. **Not an executioner**: no axe, no hood, no
   mask, no bare chest, nothing red or black.
2. `npc_gatherer_t1_front`: a townswoman gatherer: a headscarf, a plain green-brown dress with an
   apron, a wicker basket of wood and herbs on the back, a hand sickle in the front hand.
3. `npc_guard_t1_front`: a town guard: an iron cap, a padded gambeson, a round shield painted with
   the town's colours, a spear **level at chest height, tip forward**.
