# G3: the tax reeve as a parts kit (3 images)

Track: heroes. Art request 320 (Stefan, playtest run 2: "the tax collector looks like an
executioner"; 2026-10-14: "we really need to fix the tax collector"). The town's tax collector
walks from shop to shop; the game builds him from parts and animates him like the heroes
(`G1.md`, the same contract). **The gatherer and the guard come later.**

Follow `STYLE.md` (**one style**: detailed painted pixel art like the reference set, grim and
mythic, believable and buildable, no cartoon) and `docs/asset-spec.md` ("Parts kit"). Fetch raw
the reference pictures `STYLE.md` lists, above all `art-tasks/ref/hero-anchor-grim.png`: the
reeve is drawn by the same hand. **Check every image against `QA.md`** at full size before
uploading it.

**Who he is:** a **sober town official**, a reeve: a stocky middle-aged man, a trimmed grey
beard, a calm, watchful face. Plain **brown wool and leather**, a short cloak with a modest fur
collar, a belt with a ring of keys and a coin pouch. **Not an executioner:** no axe, no hood, no
mask, no bare arms, nothing red or black. Same camera and light as Grim; faces RIGHT; no effects.

## The parts sheet

One **square** image split into a **4 x 3 grid** of equal cells. One part per cell, centred,
every part fully inside its own cell, **flat magenta #FF00FF everywhere else**, all parts at the
**same scale**. Limbs drawn **straight and vertical**, near side only, round overlapping ends at
the joints.

| Row | Cells                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| --- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1   | **torso** (chest, belt with keys and pouch, the wool tunic down to just above the knees; no arms, legs or head) · **head** (face and beard, bare head, short grey hair) · **headgear** (leave empty) · **cloak** (the short cloak with its fur collar, alone, hanging) |
| 2   | **upper arm** (the sleeve) · **forearm** (sleeve end and leather cuff, no hand) · **hand** (one hand, loosely closed with a gap for a grip) · **ledger** (a thick leather-bound book with iron corners, upright)                                                       |
| 3   | **thigh** (the trousers) · **shin** (the wrapped lower leg, no boot) · **boot** (one plain leather boot, toe right) · **coin chest** (a small iron-bound wooden chest with a carry handle on top, upright)                                                             |

**Scale check** against the assembled figure height (= 100), within about 10%: head 15, torso
46, cloak 42, upper arm 22, forearm 19, hand 9, thigh 26, shin 24, boot 11, ledger 18, coin
chest 20. The torso fills about **75% of its cell's height**; everything else follows from it.
