# U1: logo and app icon, three options (6 images)

Track: ui. Art request 50. Stefan picks one option; the UI kit (U2) follows in the chosen look.

Follow `STYLE.md` for the look (chunky pixel art, thick dark outline, gritty palette: black, deep
navy, bronze-gold, worn metal). Look reference: `art-tasks/ref/chunky-warrior.png` and the concept
sheets in `docs/concept/` (fetch raw). Square canvas at your normal output size, one item per
image, nothing else in it, **flat magenta #FF00FF background**.

The game: **Godblooded**, a kingdom builder where mortal-born children of the Norse, Greek and
Egyptian gods hunt monsters. The logo carries all three pantheons.

- **Logo** (`ui_logo_<a|b|c>`): the word **GODBLOODED** as a gritty wordmark (carved, forged or
  stamped letters, bronze-gold with a dark outline) with an **emblem** that mixes a Norse, a Greek
  and an Egyptian motif. The whole logo fits the canvas width at about 90%, centred.
- **App icon** (`ui_icon_app_<a|b|c>`): the same option as a square icon: the emblem (or a bold
  "G" with the emblem) filling about 80% of the canvas, on a solid black or deep navy rounded
  square. It must **read at 48 px**: big shapes, few details, strong contrast. No small text.

The three options must differ clearly in idea, not only colour:

1. `ui_logo_a` (**option A, the shield**): a round Norse shield as the emblem, quartered by a
   Greek meander band and an Egyptian ankh at its centre; the wordmark in heavy carved
   letters below it.
2. `ui_icon_app_a`: option A as the app icon: the shield with the ankh, on black.
3. `ui_logo_b` (**option B, the three weapons**): Thor's hammer, a Greek spear and an Egyptian
   khopesh crossed behind a sun disc; the wordmark in forged bronze letters across the middle.
4. `ui_icon_app_b`: option B as the app icon: the crossed weapons and sun disc, on deep navy.
5. `ui_logo_c` (**option C, the blood drop**): a single drop of glowing gold blood (the divine
   blood of the Godbloods) framed by a raven wing, an owl feather and a falcon wing; the
   wordmark in stamped letters, a thin gold line through the double O.
6. `ui_icon_app_c`: option C as the app icon: the gold drop with the three wings, on black.
