# G1: hero parts kit on the compact rig, Grim plus faces and headgear (2 images)

Track: heroes. Art request 300 (Stefan, 2026-10-15: "I liked the small legs and arms you made. I
just didn't like the childish wizard and princess style art. If they had cool gear like on the
painterly art. Dope."). The game keeps today's **compact hero rig**: a small body with **small
floating hands and feet** (no arms or legs), animated in code. This batch **paints its pieces**
in the one style, as a kit: the game combines faces, headgear, bodies, cloaks and weapons into
many heroes. Portraits are the in-game hero drawn larger by the game (no portrait images).

Follow `STYLE.md` (**one style**: detailed painted pixel art like the reference set, grim and
mythic, believable and buildable, no cartoon) and `docs/asset-spec.md`. Fetch raw the reference
pictures `STYLE.md` lists, above all `art-tasks/ref/hero-anchor-grim.png` for Grim's face,
colours and gear. **Check every image against `QA.md`** at full size before uploading it.

- **Adult, grim, cool:** stern adult faces, believable gear (mail, leather, fur, hoods, helms,
  carved weapons). **No** wizard hats, no princess dresses, no big eyes, no blush, no cute smile.
- **Compact proportions** like today's game heroes: the head is big for the body, the body
  short and solid, the hands and boots small and separate. Readable at 40 px tall.
- Everything **faces RIGHT**, three-quarter front, light from the upper left; no effects.
