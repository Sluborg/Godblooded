Track: heroes. Art request 300 (Stefan, playtest run 2, 2026-10-14, corrected the same day:
"I still want the style we used on heroes now, just with cooler pieces of art, no svg. I don't
want static heroes"). The game builds every hero from **parts** and animates them in code (walk
bob, weapon swing, cape sway). Today those parts are simple shapes drawn by code; this batch
paints them in the one style. **Test first:** only Grim, son of Odin. Stefan sees him assembled
and moving in the game before the other three are made.

Follow `STYLE.md` (**one style**: detailed painted pixel art like the shipped longhouse and
watch tower, grim and mythic, Norse-carved, no cartoon) and `docs/asset-spec.md`. Fetch raw the
anchor pictures `STYLE.md` names and match them.

- **Grim and adult**, saga hero: stern face, small eyes, no big anime eyes, no blush, no cute
  smile, no chibi. Proportions to animate well: a sturdy body, a head a little large for
  readability (about **4 heads tall** when assembled), chunky hands and boots.
- Same game camera: 3/4 top-down seen from the lower right, the hero **faces RIGHT**.
- Readable at about 30-40 px tall assembled: strong shapes, the accent colour on cloth.
- No effects (sparks, glow, ravens in flight) in the parts: the game adds them.

## The parts sheet (`docs/asset-spec.md` "Parts sheets")

One **square** image split into a **5 x 2 grid** (5 columns, 2 rows of equal cells). One part
per cell, centred, filling at most 80% of its cell, nothing crossing into another cell, **flat
magenta #FF00FF everywhere else**. **All parts at the same scale**: the size they have when put
together. Cell order, left to right, top row first:

| Row | Cells                                                                                                                                                                         |
| --- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1   | **body** (torso and hips with the clothing, no arms, no legs) · **head** (face; the beard belongs to the head) · **headgear** (hair or hood) · **cape** · **weapon**          |
| 2   | **front foot** · **back foot** (one boot per cell) · **front hand** · **back hand** (one hand or glove per cell, **open enough to hold a grip**) · **shield** (empty if none) |

- The **body** shows where the arms attach (shoulders) but has no arms: the hands float, as
  in the game today.
- The **front hand holds nothing** in the sheet; draw it as a loosely closed fist with a hole
  for the weapon's grip.
- The **weapon** alone, upright, grip at the bottom, the whole weapon inside its cell.
