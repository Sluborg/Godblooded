# G1: hero parts kit, test sheet with Grim son of Odin (4 images)

Track: heroes. Art request 300 (Stefan, playtest run 2, 2026-10-14). Stefan approved the look of
Grim (`art-tasks/ref/hero-anchor-grim.png`) and wants heroes **animated from painted parts**:
the game builds each hero from separate body segments (with real arms and legs that bend at
elbow and knee) and moves them in code; later it combines heads, bodies, gear and weapons into
many heroes. **This batch is the test:** Grim split into his segments, a second weapon, that
weapon's icon, and Grim's portrait. The full kit comes after Stefan has seen Grim moving in the game.

Follow `STYLE.md` (**one style**: detailed painted pixel art like the shipped longhouse and
watch tower, grim and mythic, Norse-carved, no cartoon) and `docs/asset-spec.md`. Fetch raw the
anchor pictures `STYLE.md` names and match them; above all the hero anchor
`art-tasks/ref/hero-anchor-grim.png`.

- **Exactly Grim's look**: the same face, hood, cloak, leather and knotwork, staff, colours,
  outline and level of detail as the anchor picture.
- Every segment **faces RIGHT** (3/4 front, as in the anchor), light from the upper left.
- **Limbs drawn straight**, hanging or standing, never bent: the game bends them.
- Draw only the **near side** limbs (the game reuses them for the far side, a little darker).
- No effects (glow, sparks, ravens in flight) in any part: the game adds them.

## The parts sheet

One **square** image split into a **4 x 3 grid** of equal cells (4 columns, 3 rows). One part per
cell, centred, nothing crossing into another cell, **flat magenta #FF00FF everywhere else**.
**All parts at the same scale**, the size they have together: the staff, the tallest part, fills
about **85% of its cell's height**, and every other part is drawn to match it (assembled, Grim
would be about as tall as his staff). Cell order, left to right, top row first:

| Row | Cells                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| --- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1   | **torso** (chest and hips with clothing and belt; no arms, no legs, no head) · **head** (face and beard, bare: no hood) · **headgear** (the hood alone, open where the face goes) · **cloak** (alone, hanging, with its raven feathers)                                                            |
| 2   | **upper arm** (shoulder to elbow, the sleeve) · **forearm** (elbow to fingertips: sleeve, leather bracer and hand, the hand a loosely closed fist with a gap for a grip) · **thigh** (hip to knee, the trousers) · **shin** (knee to sole: the wrapped lower leg and the boot, toe pointing right) |
| 3   | **staff** (his gnarled ash staff, iron bands, iron-ringed head) · **axe** (a Norse bearded axe: dark oak haft with iron bands, heavy iron head) · **off-hand** (leave empty: Grim has none) · **empty**                                                                                            |

- **Weapons point straight UP**, tip at the top of the cell, the grip in the lower part; the game
  rotates them.
- The limb segments are **vertical**, top joint at the top of the picture, bottom joint at the
  bottom.
- Where segments meet (shoulder, elbow, hip, knee, neck), let each piece **overlap a little**
  past the joint (round ends), so no gap shows when the game bends the limb.
