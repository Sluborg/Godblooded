# G1: hero parts kit, test sheet with Grim son of Odin (4 images)

Track: heroes. Art request 300 (Stefan, playtest run 2, 2026-10-14). Stefan approved the look of
Grim (`art-tasks/ref/hero-anchor-grim.png`) and wants heroes **animated from painted parts**:
the game builds each hero from separate parts and moves them in code (walk bob, weapon swing,
cape sway), and later combines heads, bodies, gear and weapons into many heroes. **This batch is
the test:** Grim split into his parts, one swapped weapon and its icon. The full kit comes after
Stefan has seen Grim moving in the game.

Follow `STYLE.md` (**one style**: detailed painted pixel art like the shipped longhouse and
watch tower, grim and mythic, Norse-carved, no cartoon) and `docs/asset-spec.md`. Fetch raw the
anchor pictures `STYLE.md` names and match them; above all the hero anchor
`art-tasks/ref/hero-anchor-grim.png`.

- **Exactly Grim's look**: the same face, hood, cloak, leather and knotwork, staff, colours,
  outline and level of detail as the anchor picture.
- Same camera: 3/4 top-down seen from the lower right, **facing RIGHT**.
- No effects (glow, sparks, ravens in flight) in any part: the game adds them.

## The parts sheet

One **square** image split into a **5 x 2 grid** of equal cells. One part per cell, centred,
filling at most 80% of its cell, nothing crossing into another cell, **flat magenta #FF00FF
everywhere else**. **All parts at the same scale**: assembled, Grim would stand about **2 cells tall**. Cell order, left to right, top row first:

| Row | Cells                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| --- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1   | **body** (torso and hips with clothing and belt, **no arms, no legs**) · **head** (face and beard, bare, no hood) · **headgear** (the hood alone, open where the face goes) · **cape** (the cloak alone, hanging, with its raven feathers) · **weapon** |
| 2   | **front foot** · **back foot** (one boot with its lower leg wrap per cell) · **front hand** · **back hand** (one hand with its leather bracer per cell) · **shield** (leave empty)                                                                      |

- **No arms:** the game floats the hands beside the body (like the heroes today). Each hand
  part includes the forearm bracer so it reads as an arm end.
- **Front hand:** a loosely closed fist with a gap for a grip, holding nothing. **Back hand:** a
  relaxed fist.
- **Weapon:** alone, **pointing straight UP** (tip at the top of the cell), the grip in the lower
  third. The game rotates it.
