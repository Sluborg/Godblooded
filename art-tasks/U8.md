# U8: UI kit in carved wood, panel, buttons and icons (13 images)

Track: ui. Art request 260 (Stefan's taste test, then playtest run 2, 2026-10-14: the title
buttons are ugly; one style for everything). Redraws the U2 kit; the new pictures replace the old
ones under the same ids.

Follow `STYLE.md` (**one style**: detailed painted pixel art like the shipped longhouse and
watch tower, grim and mythic, Norse-carved, no cartoon) and `docs/asset-spec.md`. Fetch raw the
anchor pictures `STYLE.md` names and match them. Square canvas at your normal output size, one
item per image, nothing else in it, **flat magenta #FF00FF background**. No text and no letters
anywhere.

**Materials:** frames of **dark weathered oak** like the longhouse beams, carved with knotwork,
held by **dark iron corner brackets** with rivets; panels of **aged parchment** (cream to ochre,
darker worn edges). Same thin dark-brown outline as the anchors.

## Panel and buttons (stretched by the game)

The game stretches the panel and the buttons to any size with **9-slice**: the four corners stay
as drawn, the edges stretch along their length, the middle stretches both ways. So:

- All ornament (pegs, carved corner pieces, knots) sits **in the corners only**, each corner
  inside the outer **15%** of the shape's width and height.
- The four edges between the corners are a **plain, even wooden band** (same thickness, no
  ornament, no change along its length) so they stretch without smearing.
- The middle is an **even parchment fill** (a faint paper grain is fine, no pattern, no picture).

1. `ui_panel`: a dialog panel: a dark carved oak frame with iron corner brackets, the middle
   plain aged parchment. The panel fills about **94% of the canvas** both ways, centred.
2. `ui_button`: a button in its normal state: a wide bar about **90% of the canvas width and 32%
   of its height**, centred: a dark oak plank frame with an iron bracket in each corner, the
   middle a lighter worn-wood fill, a soft light top edge (raised).
3. `ui_button_pressed`: **an edit of item 2**, same canvas, size and position: pushed in: the
   middle a shade darker, the light edge moved to the bottom (sunken), the brackets unchanged.
4. `ui_button_disabled`: **an edit of item 2**, same canvas, size and position: greyed out:
   faded grey-brown wood, a flat dull middle, no highlight.

## Icons (drawn on top of buttons and the HUD)

Each icon is a **single bold symbol** filling about **80% of the canvas**, centred, the anchors'
thin dark-brown outline, no plate or frame behind it. It must **read at 32 px**: big shapes, two
or three warm colours, no fine detail.

5. `ui_icon_gold`: a small stack of three gold coins, one coin standing in front.
6. `ui_icon_speed1`: one bold play-arrow (triangle pointing right), carved wood with a gold edge.
7. `ui_icon_speed2`: two play-arrows side by side, overlapping a little.
8. `ui_icon_speed4`: three play-arrows side by side, overlapping, with a warm orange edge.
9. `ui_icon_menu`: three thick wooden bars.
10. `ui_icon_bounty`: the bounty flag as in `B13.md` item 2: a carved dark pole with a small

11. `ui_icon_upgrade`: a thick arrow pointing up, a small smith's hammer crossed behind it.
