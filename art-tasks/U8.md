# U8: UI kit in wood and parchment, panel, buttons and icons (13 images)

Track: ui. Art request 260 (Stefan's taste test, `docs/decisions.md` 2026-10-14: wood-and-parchment
UI frames). Redraws the U2 kit; the new pictures replace the old ones under the same ids.

Follow `STYLE.md` (big-head cartoon world, **thin colour-matched outlines, painterly shading,
warm earthy muted palette**). Square canvas at your normal output size, one item per image,
nothing else in it, **flat magenta #FF00FF background**. No text and no letters anywhere.

**Materials:** frames of **warm carved wood** (oak and walnut browns, a little worn, small iron
nails or pegs), panels of **parchment** (cream to light ochre, soft painterly texture, slightly
darker edges). No bronze, no dark iron, no navy.

## Panel and buttons (stretched by the game)

The game stretches the panel and the buttons to any size with **9-slice**: the four corners stay
as drawn, the edges stretch along their length, the middle stretches both ways. So:

- All ornament (pegs, carved corner pieces, knots) sits **in the corners only**, each corner
  inside the outer **15%** of the shape's width and height.
- The four edges between the corners are a **plain, even wooden band** (same thickness, no
  ornament, no change along its length) so they stretch without smearing.
- The middle is an **even parchment fill** (a faint paper grain is fine, no pattern, no picture).

1. `ui_panel`: a dialog panel: a carved wooden frame with small pegged corner pieces, the middle
   plain parchment. The panel fills about **94% of the canvas** both ways, centred.
2. `ui_button`: a button in its normal state: a wide bar about **90% of the canvas width and 32%
   of its height**, centred: a rounded wooden plank frame with a peg in each corner, the middle
   a lighter parchment-wood fill, a soft light top edge (raised).
3. `ui_button_pressed`: **an edit of item 2**, same canvas, size and position: pushed in: the
   middle a shade darker, the light edge moved to the bottom (sunken), the pegs unchanged.
4. `ui_button_disabled`: **an edit of item 2**, same canvas, size and position: greyed out:
   faded grey-brown wood, a flat dull middle, no highlight.

## Icons (drawn on top of buttons and the HUD)

Each icon is a **single bold symbol** filling about **80% of the canvas**, centred, thin
colour-matched outline, no plate or frame behind it. It must **read at 32 px**: big shapes, two
or three warm colours, no fine detail.
