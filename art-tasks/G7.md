# G7: hero look test on the compact rig (8 images)

Track: heroes. Art request 450 (Stefan, 2026-10-15: "Proportions are fine. Have heroes make some
test art so I can describe what works."). A quick look test before the kit sheet: eight
assembled heroes, each a different mix, so Stefan can point at what works. Nothing ships from
this batch.

Follow `STYLE.md` (**one style**: detailed painted pixel art like the reference set, grim and
mythic, believable gear, no cartoon) and fetch raw `art-tasks/ref/hero-anchor-grim.png` for the
faces, materials, colours and level of detail. **Check every image against `QA.md`** at full size
before uploading it.

**Proportions: today's game heroes**, not the tall anchor: fetch raw
`docs/reference/style-test-1.png` and copy **only the proportions** of the four small heroes
(Grim, Astrid, Eirik, Narfi): a big head, a short solid body, **small separate hands and boots
that float** beside and under the body (no arms, no legs drawn), about **2.5 heads tall**. Copy
nothing else from that picture: not its thick outlines, flat colours, wizard hat, princess dress
or cute faces.

- **Adult, grim, cool:** stern adult faces, small eyes, real gear (mail, leather, fur, hoods,
  helms, carved weapons), weathered. No wizard hats, no gowns, no blush, no smiles.
- Square canvas, one hero per image, **flat magenta #FF00FF background**, figure about **75% of
  the canvas height**, centred, feet at about 88%. **Front view**, a slight three-quarter turn to
  the right. The weapon held in the front hand, the whole weapon inside the picture. No effects.

1. `g7_grim`: **Grim, son of Odin**: grey-blue hood and cloak with raven feathers, leather vest
   with knotwork, a gnarled ash staff with iron bands.
2. `g7_axeman`: **a red-braided axeman**: braided red beard, mail shirt over a dark tunic, a
   bearded axe.
3. `g7_oldwarrior`: **a grey old warrior**: grey beard, an iron spangenhelm with a nasal guard, a
   round wooden shield with iron boss on the back hand, a short sword.
4. `g7_shieldmaiden`: **a stern blonde shieldmaiden-seeress**: tight blonde war braids, fur-trimmed
   dark wool coat over leather, amber necklace, a carved staff with falcon feathers. Fierce, not
   a princess.
5. `g7_lokison`: **a son of Loki**: lean and sly, a fur cap with an iron band, dark green and
   leather, two seax knives (one in each floating hand).
6. `g7_thorson`: **a son of Thor**: broad, heavy fur mantle over mail, an iron helm, a short
   war hammer.
7. `g7_bold_a`: **a bolder variant of the axeman**: darker palette (blackened mail, deep red
   cloth), richer trim (silver knotwork, a carved bone clasp).
8. `g7_bold_b`: **a bolder variant of Grim**: near-black cloak, a raven-skull clasp, silver runes
   on the staff, a single faint pale rune on the vest (no glow).
