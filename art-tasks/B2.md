# B2: heroes tier 1, ranger and wizard (4 images)

Track: heroes. Art request 10.

Follow `STYLE.md` (classic fantasy strategy, about 4.5 heads tall, weathered, painted) and
`docs/asset-spec.md` (framing, views). Match the look of the reference warrior
`art-tasks/ref/hero_warrior_t1_front.png` (fetch it raw): same proportions, outline, shading and
camera. Square canvas at your normal output size, one item per image, nothing else in it.

Units: front = faces the camera, turned 3/4 toward the lower right; back = **the same
character** from behind, turned 3/4 toward the upper right. Whole figure visible, feet at about
88% of the canvas height, figure about 75% of the canvas height, centred, feet planted in a ready
stance. Tier 1 = fresh recruit: worn leather and iron, muted earthy colours, plus the class colour
on a big area.

1. `hero_ranger_t1_front`: a young Godblood ranger. **Class colour: deep forest green** hooded
   cloak. Leather jerkin, a longbow held by the middle in the left hand, a quiver of arrows on the
   back, a knife on the belt. Lean, alert, weathered face. **Flat magenta #FF00FF background**
   (the cloak is green).
2. `hero_ranger_t1_back`: the same ranger from behind; cloak, quiver and bow read clearly.
   **Flat magenta #FF00FF background.**
3. `hero_wizard_t1_front`: a young Godblood wizard. **Class colour: deep purple** robe with a
   hood down on the shoulders, rope belt, a satchel of scrolls, a gnarled wooden staff taller than
   the wizard topped with a small pale crystal, held upright in the right hand. Thin, intense,
   ink-stained fingers. Flat green #00FF00 background.
4. `hero_wizard_t1_back`: the same wizard from behind; robe, satchel and staff read clearly. Flat
   green #00FF00 background.
