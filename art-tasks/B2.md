# B2: heroes tier 1, ranger and wizard (4 images)

Track: heroes. Art request 10.

Follow `STYLE.md` (chunky pixel art, faces right, weapon placed by the strike) and
`docs/asset-spec.md`. **Look reference: `art-tasks/ref/chunky-warrior.png`** (fetch it raw): match
its proportions (about 3.5 to 4 heads, big head, big hands and feet, oversized weapon), thick dark
outline, pixel shading and camera. More chunky references in `art-tasks/ref/` (`chunky-*.png`),
concept sheets in `docs/concept/`. Square canvas at your normal output size, one item per image,
nothing else in it.

Every unit is **one still picture per view**, no frames: the game does all motion in code.

- **front:** faces RIGHT, a three-quarter view toward the lower right.
- **back:** **the same character** from behind, turned three-quarter toward the upper right; the
  weapon in the same strike position, seen from behind.
- Whole figure visible, feet at about 88% of the canvas height, figure about 75% of the canvas
  height, centred, wide planted stance. Nothing cut off, the whole weapon inside the picture.
- **Strike** (table in `STYLE.md`): it fixes where the weapon is drawn, so the game's strike
  starts there. Each item names it and spells out the pose.

Tier 1 = fresh recruit: worn leather and iron, scuffed and dark, no glow; the class colour on a
big area so the class reads at phone size.

1. `hero_ranger_t1_front` (**strike: shot**): a young Godblood ranger. **Class colour: deep forest
   green** hooded cloak. Leather jerkin, a quiver of arrows on the back, a knife on the belt.
   **Pose:** a big longbow held in front of the body at chest height, an arrow nocked, pointing
   right. Lean, alert, weathered face. **Flat magenta #FF00FF background.**
2. `hero_ranger_t1_back` (**strike: shot**): the same ranger from behind, the bow in the same
   place; cloak, quiver and bow read clearly. **Flat magenta #FF00FF background.**
3. `hero_wizard_t1_front` (**strike: bolt**): a young Godblood wizard. **Class colour: deep
   purple** robe with the hood down on the shoulders, rope belt, a satchel of scrolls. **Pose:** a
   gnarled wooden staff taller than the wizard, topped with a small pale crystal, held in the
   **FRONT hand** (the hand nearer the right edge), its crystal raised at shoulder height in front
   of the body; the other hand free behind, a faint spark in the palm. Thin, intense, ink-stained
   fingers. Flat green #00FF00 background.
4. `hero_wizard_t1_back` (**strike: bolt**): the same wizard from behind, the staff in the same
   front hand and place; robe, satchel and staff read clearly. Flat green #00FF00 background.
