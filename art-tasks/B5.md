# B5: monsters tier 1, minotaur, jackal-man, wyrm (6 images)

Track: monsters. Art request 20. Monsters are spooky but cute, never gory, and lean darker and
greener than heroes.

Follow `STYLE.md` and `docs/asset-spec.md` (framing, views). Square canvas at your normal output
size, one item per image, nothing else in it. **Match the look of the B1 images** (shipped in
`public/assets/units/` once approved): same chibi proportions, outline, shading and camera.

Units: front = faces the camera, turned 3/4 toward the lower right; back = **the same
character** from behind, turned 3/4 toward the upper right. Whole figure visible, feet at about
88% of the canvas height, figure about 75% of the canvas height, centred. Tier 1 = fresh: worn
leather and iron, muted colours, few big shapes.

1. `mon_minotaur_t1_front`: a Greek minotaur: a bull head with big curved horns and a nose ring
   on a stocky chibi body, brown fur, a leather kilt, an oversized double-headed bronze axe.
   Snorting, angry eyebrows. Flat green #00FF00 background.
2. `mon_minotaur_t1_back`: the same minotaur from behind; horns and axe read clearly. Flat green
   #00FF00 background.
3. `mon_jackal-man_t1_front`: an Egyptian jackal-man: a jackal head with tall pointed ears and
   black fur, a white linen kilt, a plain copper collar, an oversized curved khopesh sword.
   Watchful, sneering. Flat green #00FF00 background.
4. `mon_jackal-man_t1_back`: the same jackal-man from behind; ears, tail and khopesh read
   clearly. Flat green #00FF00 background.
5. `mon_wyrm_t1_front`: a Norse wyrm, the boss: a wingless serpent-dragon, its long body coiled
   on the ground with the head and neck raised high, dark teal scales, a pale belly, small
   horns, glowing amber eyes, a toothy but cute snarl. The whole coil fits the unit framing (the
   coil's lowest point on the feet line). **Flat magenta #FF00FF background.**
6. `mon_wyrm_t1_back`: the same wyrm from behind, coil and raised neck. **Flat magenta #FF00FF
   background.**
