# B5: monsters tier 1, minotaur, jackal-man, wyrm (3 images)

Track: monsters. Art request 20.

Follow `STYLE.md` (chunky pixel art, faces right, weapon placed by the strike) and
`docs/asset-spec.md`. **Look reference: `art-tasks/ref/chunky-warrior.png`** (fetch it raw): match
its proportions (about 3.5 to 4 heads, big head, big hands and feet, oversized weapon), thick dark
outline, pixel shading and camera. More chunky references in `art-tasks/ref/` (`chunky-*.png`),
concept sheets in `docs/concept/`. Square canvas at your normal output size, one item per image,
nothing else in it.

Every unit is **one front picture**, no frames and no back view: the game mirrors it and does
all motion in code. Tier 1 only: the game shows tougher monsters by colour.

- **front:** faces RIGHT, a three-quarter view toward the lower right.
- Whole figure visible, feet at about 88% of the canvas height, figure about 75% of the canvas
  height, centred, wide planted stance. Nothing cut off, the whole weapon inside the picture.
- **Strike** (table in `STYLE.md`): it fixes where the weapon is drawn, so the game's strike
  starts there. Each item names it and spells out the pose.

Monsters are grim and dangerous, never cute or gory, and lean darker and greener than heroes.
Tier 1 = the common kind: no glow except where an item says so.

1. `mon_minotaur_t1_front` (**strike: overhead chop**): a Greek minotaur: a bull head with big
   curved horns and a nose ring on a massive body, brown fur, a leather kilt. **Pose:** an
   oversized double-headed bronze axe **raised high behind the back shoulder**, gripped at the
   lower end. Snorting, furious. **Flat magenta #FF00FF background.**
2. `mon_jackal-man_t1_front` (**strike: side sweep**): an Egyptian jackal-man: a jackal head with
   tall pointed ears and black fur, a white linen kilt, a plain copper collar. **Pose:** a curved
   khopesh sword **held level behind the hip**, ready to sweep forward. Watchful, sneering. **Flat magenta #FF00FF background.**
3. `mon_wyrm_t1_front` (**strike: thrust**): a Norse wyrm, the boss: a wingless serpent-dragon,
   its long body coiled on the ground, dark teal scales, a pale belly, horns, amber eyes. **Pose:**
   the head and neck **level at chest height, jaws open and pointing right**, ready to strike
   forward like a spear. The whole coil fits the unit framing (the coil's lowest point on the
   feet line). **Flat magenta #FF00FF background.**
