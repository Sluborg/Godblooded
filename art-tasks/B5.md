# B5: monsters tier 1, minotaur, jackal-man, wyrm (6 images)

Track: monsters. Art request 20. Monsters are grim and dangerous, never cute or gory, and lean darker and greener than heroes.

Follow `STYLE.md` (classic fantasy strategy, about 4.5 heads tall, weathered, painted) and
`docs/asset-spec.md` (framing, views). Match the look of the reference warrior
`art-tasks/ref/hero_warrior_t1_front.png` (fetch it raw): same proportions, outline, shading and
camera. Square canvas at your normal output size, one item per image, nothing else in it.

Units: front = faces the camera, turned 3/4 toward the lower right; back = **the same
character** from behind, turned 3/4 toward the upper right. Whole figure visible, feet at about
88% of the canvas height, figure about 75% of the canvas height, centred, feet planted in a ready
stance. Tier 1 = fresh recruit: worn leather and iron, muted earthy colours, plus the class colour
on a big area.

Each unit's **strike** sets where its weapon is drawn (table in `STYLE.md`). This draft is
rewritten for the chunky look and the face-right pose standard before it goes Ready.

1. `mon_minotaur_t1_front` (**strike: overhead chop**): a Greek minotaur: a bull head with big curved horns and a nose ring
   on a massive body, brown fur, a leather kilt, a double-headed bronze axe gripped at the lower
   end. Snorting, furious. Flat green #00FF00 background.
2. `mon_minotaur_t1_back` (**strike: overhead chop**): the same minotaur from behind; horns and axe read clearly. Flat green
   #00FF00 background.
3. `mon_jackal-man_t1_front` (**strike: side sweep**): an Egyptian jackal-man: a jackal head with tall pointed ears and
   black fur, a white linen kilt, a plain copper collar, a curved khopesh sword held by the hilt.
   Watchful, sneering. Flat green #00FF00 background.
4. `mon_jackal-man_t1_back` (**strike: side sweep**): the same jackal-man from behind; ears, tail and khopesh read clearly.
   Flat green #00FF00 background.
5. `mon_wyrm_t1_front` (**strike: thrust**): a Norse wyrm, the boss: a wingless serpent-dragon, its long body coiled on
   the ground with the head and neck raised high, dark teal scales, a pale belly, horns, amber
   eyes, a snarl full of teeth. The whole coil fits the unit framing (the coil's lowest point on
   the feet line). **Flat magenta #FF00FF background.**
6. `mon_wyrm_t1_back` (**strike: thrust**): the same wyrm from behind, coil and raised neck. **Flat magenta #FF00FF
   background.**
