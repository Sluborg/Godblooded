**Start from your own U1 pictures** `ui_logo_b` and `ui_icon_app_b` (in this chat, or in the Drive
folder as `U1--ui_logo_b.png` and `U1--ui_icon_app_b.png`): same layout, same chunky pixel style,

  spearhead** on top, a plain wooden shaft and a small bronze butt-spike (sauroter) at the bottom;
  no second point, no rings along the shaft. A short red horsehair tassel under the head is fine.
- **Sun behind:** calmer. **Fewer, shorter, rounder rays** (about 12 short wedges, not long
  spikes), the disc a warm bronze-gold, so the weapons and the wordmark stand out.
