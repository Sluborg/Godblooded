body (no arms, no legs drawn), painted in the one style (`STYLE.md`), grim, believable gear. Same
scale family as the heroes of `G7.md` and the G10 minotaur. Square canvas, **flat magenta

height, feet at about 88%. Parts sheets: a 3 x 2 grid, each part centred in its cell with space
around it, nothing touching, the same colours, outline and light as the assembled figure.

   held in the front fist, pointing up.
