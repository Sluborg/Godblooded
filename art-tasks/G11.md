# G11: compact-rig monsters: draugr and troll, whole and parts (4 images)

Track: monsters. Art request 550 (Stefan, 2026-10-16: the compact minotaur G10 approved, rated 5;
"order the other monsters on the same compact rig", starting with the earliest to spawn).
Look tests that also give Scene the parts; Art ships the parts after Stefan's rating.

Exactly the rig and look of **G10** (fetch `art-tasks/G10.md` raw from main and follow it): a
**big head, a short solid body, small separate hands and feet that float** beside and under the
body (no arms, no legs drawn), painted in the one style (`STYLE.md`), grim, believable gear. Same
scale family as the heroes of `G7.md` and the G10 minotaur. Square canvas, **flat magenta
#FF00FF background**. **Check against `QA.md`** at full size (rules 1, 3, 10, 11; reads at 40 px).
Assembled figures: front view, a slight three-quarter turn to the right, about 75% of the canvas
height, feet at about 88%. Parts sheets: a 3 x 2 grid, each part centred in its cell with space
around it, nothing touching, the same colours, outline and light as the assembled figure.

**Drawn for animation** (Stefan, 2026-10-16; the game moves every part on its own): each **head**
ends in a short neck and collar stub that tucks under the body's neckline (the body has no neck
of its own), so it can bob and turn with no seam; every **boot or foot** points its toe forward to
the right, the way the monster walks, never splayed; every **weapon** is long and whole with the
**lower third of the handle bare** so the fist grips it low for a big overhead chop.

1. `g11_draugr`: a **draugr** (undead Norse warrior), about the height of a hero: a gaunt
   grey-blue dead face with a sparse beard and pale glowing eyes, a rusted iron helm, a torn mail
   shirt over rotten cloth, bony floating fists, worn iron-shod boots, a **rusty bearded axe**
   held in the front fist, pointing up.
2. `kit_draugr`: the same draugr as parts: row 1 **head** (with the helm), **body** (torso with
   mail and cloth, no head, no limbs), **hand** (one fist; its grip comes from item 5); row 2 **boot** (one),
   **axe** (pointing up), an empty cell.
3. `g11_troll`: a **troll**, clearly bigger and heavier than the minotaur (about a head taller,
   much broader): a huge mossy head with a long nose, small eyes and tusks, a hunched furred and
   mossy body with a hide loincloth and a rope belt, two huge floating fists, two big bare
   floating feet, a **rough tree-trunk club** in the front fist, pointing up.
4. `kit_troll`: the same troll as parts: row 1 **head**, **body**, **hand** (one fist; its grip comes from
   item 5); row 2 **foot** (one), **club** (pointing up), an empty cell.
