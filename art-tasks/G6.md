# G6: town folk, gatherer and guard as parts kits (4 images)

Track: heroes. Art request 440 (Stefan's "go with all", 2026-10-14). The rest of the town folk
after the reeve (`G3.md`): they walk animated like the heroes. **Run it after G3.**

Follow `STYLE.md` (**one style**: detailed painted pixel art like the reference set, grim and
mythic, believable and buildable, no cartoon) and `docs/asset-spec.md` ("Parts kit"). Fetch raw
the reference pictures `STYLE.md` lists, above all `art-tasks/ref/hero-anchor-grim.png`: town
folk are drawn by the same hand. **Check every image against `QA.md`** at full size before
uploading it.

Each sheet: one **square** image, a **4 x 3 grid** of equal cells, one part per cell, centred,
fully inside its cell, **flat magenta #FF00FF everywhere else**, all parts at the **same
scale**; limbs straight and vertical, near side only, round overlapping ends at the joints. Cell
order as in `G3.md` (torso, head, headgear, cloak / upper arm, forearm, hand, main-hand item /
thigh, shin, boot, off-hand item); leave a cell empty if the person has no such part. Scale as
in `G3.md` (against the assembled figure height = 100: head 15, torso 46, upper arm 22, forearm
19, hand 9, thigh 26, shin 24, boot 11); the torso fills about 75% of its cell's height.

Ordinary Norse townsfolk, plainer and smaller in presence than heroes, adult faces, no cartoon.
Faces right; no effects.

1. `kit_gatherer`: a **townswoman gatherer**: a headscarf (headgear cell), a plain green-brown
   wool dress with an apron (torso), no cloak (leave empty), a hand sickle (main-hand cell, blade
   up), a wicker basket of wood and herbs (off-hand cell, carried by its handle).
2. `por_gatherer`: her portrait: head and shoulders, the same face and headscarf, facing right
   three-quarter, the head about half the canvas height, **flat magenta #FF00FF background**.
3. `kit_guard`: a **town guard**: an iron cap (headgear), a padded gambeson with a leather belt
   (torso), a short dark cloak, a spear (main-hand cell, pointing straight up), a round wooden
   shield painted with plain town colours, dull blue and cream (off-hand cell).
4. `por_guard`: his portrait: head and shoulders, the same face and iron cap, facing right
   three-quarter, **flat magenta #FF00FF background**.
