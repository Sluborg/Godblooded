## Units as parts (items 1-4)

The game will build each unit from separate **parts** and move them in code (walk bob, swing,
cape sway), like the reference prototype. A **parts sheet** is one square image split into a
**5 x 2 grid** (5 columns, 2 rows): one part in each cell, centred, filling at most 80% of its
cell, nothing crossing into another cell, **flat magenta #FF00FF everywhere else**. All parts on
a sheet are drawn **at the same scale**, the size they have when put together.

Grid order (left to right, top row then bottom row), as in `docs/asset-spec.md` "Parts
sheets": **body** (torso with the clothing, no arms or legs) · **head** (face, bare; a beard
belongs to the head) · **headgear** (hair, hood or helmet, sits on the head) · **cape** (alone,
hanging) · **weapon** (alone, held by its grip) / **front foot** · **back foot** (one foot or boot
per cell) · **front hand** · **back hand** (one round, simple hand or glove per cell) · **shield**
(alone; leave the cell empty magenta if the unit has none). No effects (sparks, glow) in the
parts: the game adds them.
