# S5: style test, cartoon units from parts (8 images, priority)

Track: heroes (this chat does all 8, buildings included). Art request 220 (Stefan via Lead
2026-10-13). **Do this batch first.** It tests a new look; nothing ships into the game yet. Upload as usual
(`S5--<id>.png`); the ids start with `s5_` so they never clash with game ids.

**This batch does NOT follow `STYLE.md`.** New direction to test:

- **Cartoon, RimWorld-like:** simple rounded shapes, **flat colours** (one base colour per area,
  at most one shade step, no gradients, no texture, no pixel-art dithering), a **thick even dark
  outline** around every part (about 2% of the canvas width), clean smooth edges.
- **Readable at about 30 px tall:** big round head, small simple body, chunky weapon, strong
  colour blocks. No fine detail that disappears when small.
- Light from the top left, but only as one flat lighter shade, never a gradient.
- Same game camera as before: 3/4 top-down, seen from the lower right, figures **face RIGHT**.
- No text, no ground or shadow unless an item asks for it.

## Units as parts (items 1-4)

The game will build each unit from separate **parts** and move them in code (walk bob, swing,
cape sway). A **parts sheet** is one square image split into a **3 x 2 grid** (3 columns, 2
rows): one part in each cell, centred, filling at most 80% of its cell, nothing crossing into
another cell, **flat magenta #FF00FF everywhere else**. All parts on a sheet are drawn **at the
same scale**, the size they have when put together.

Grid order (left to right, top row then bottom row):
**body** (torso, arms and legs, no head) · **head** (face, bare) · **hair or helmet** (sits on
the head) · **weapon** (alone, held by its grip) · **shield** (alone) · **cape** (alone, hanging).

1. `s5_hero_thor_parts`: **Daughter of Thor**, the hero from `public/assets/units/
hero_thor_t1_front.png` (fetch raw for colours and outfit) redrawn in the new style:
   red-blonde braids (hair part), storm-blue tunic and leather on the body, a one-handed bearded
   axe (weapon), a round wooden shield with a blue lightning mark, a short grey fur cape.
2. `s5_hero_thor_whole`: the **same parts assembled** into the whole hero, standing, facing
   right, axe raised behind the back shoulder, shield on the front arm, figure about 75% of the
   canvas height, feet at about 88%. **Flat magenta #FF00FF background.** This shows how the
   parts fit together.
3. `s5_mon_draugr_parts`: **draugr** (undead Norse warrior, from
   `public/assets/units/mon_draugr_t1_front.png`) in the new style: grey-green skin and rusted
   mail on the body, a skull-like head with glowing blue eyes, a dented iron helmet, a notched
   sword, a cracked round shield, a tattered dark cape.
4. `s5_mon_draugr_whole`: the same draugr parts assembled, facing right, sword raised,
   figure about 75% of the canvas height, feet at about 88%. **Flat magenta #FF00FF
   background.**
