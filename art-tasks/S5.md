## Units as parts (items 1-4)

The game will build each unit from separate **parts** and move them in code (walk bob, swing,
cape sway), like the reference prototype. A **parts sheet** is one square image split into a
**4 x 2 grid** (4 columns, 2 rows): one part in each cell, centred, filling at most 80% of its
cell, nothing crossing into another cell, **flat magenta #FF00FF everywhere else**. All parts on
a sheet are drawn **at the same scale**, the size they have when put together.

Grid order (left to right, top row then bottom row):
**body** (torso with the clothing, no arms or legs) · **head** (face, bare) · **headgear** (hair,
hood or helmet, sits on the head) · **cape** (alone, hanging) · **feet** (the two feet or boots
side by side) · **hands** (the two hands or gloves side by side, round and simple) · **weapon**
(alone, held by its grip) · **shield** (alone; leave the cell empty magenta if the unit has none).
