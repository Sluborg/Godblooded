# U6: level-up card frames, family icons and pantheon sigils (11 images)

Track: ui. Art request 150 (backlog Art 150). Use the U8 **wood and parchment** materials (`art-tasks/U8.md`), in the look of
`STYLE.md` (thin colour-matched outlines, painterly shading, warm, slightly muted palette). Design: `docs/systems/progression.md` ("Families", level-up card).

Follow `STYLE.md`. Square canvas at your normal output size, one item per image, **flat magenta
#FF00FF background**, no text and no letters.

## Card frames (stretched by the game, 9-slice)

A level-up card is a tall card; the game stretches the frame to size with **9-slice**, exactly
as the U8 panel: ornament only in the **four corners** (inside the outer 15% of the shape), the
four edges a **plain even band**, the middle a **flat even fill**. Each frame is a **portrait
card about 60% of the canvas wide and 90% tall**, centred, the three **identical in shape**,
only material and colour change.

1. `ui_card_iron`: common: a plain wooden frame with small iron corner pegs, a parchment middle.
2. `ui_card_bronze`: rare: **an edit of item 1**, same shape: the wooden frame with bronze corner
   caps, a warmer parchment middle.
3. `ui_card_gold`: epic: **an edit of item 1**, same shape: the wooden frame with gilded carved
   corner pieces and a small gem in each corner, a golden-cream parchment middle.

## Family icons (top corner of a card)
