# B4: monsters tier 1, draugr, troll and harpy (6 images)

Track: monsters. Art request 20. Monsters are grim and dangerous, never cute or gory, and lean darker and greener than heroes.

Follow `STYLE.md` (classic fantasy strategy, about 4.5 heads tall, weathered, painted) and
`docs/asset-spec.md` (framing, views). Match the look of the reference warrior
`art-tasks/ref/hero_warrior_t1_front.png` (fetch it raw): same proportions, outline, shading and
camera. Square canvas at your normal output size, one item per image, nothing else in it.

Units: front = faces the camera, turned 3/4 toward the lower right; back = **the same
character** from behind, turned 3/4 toward the upper right. Whole figure visible, feet at about
88% of the canvas height, figure about 75% of the canvas height, centred, feet planted in a ready
stance. Tier 1 = fresh recruit: worn leather and iron, muted earthy colours, plus the class colour
on a big area.

Each unit's **strike** sets where its weapon is drawn (table in `STYLE.md`). This draft is
rewritten for the chunky look and the face-right pose standard before it goes Ready.

1. `mon_draugr_t1_front` (**strike: overhead chop**): a draugr, an undead Norse warrior risen from a burial mound: gaunt,
   grey-green dead skin, pale glowing eyes, a rusty dented helmet, a torn dark cloak, a notched
   old sword held by the hilt. **Flat magenta #FF00FF background** (the skin is green).
2. `mon_draugr_t1_back` (**strike: overhead chop**): the same draugr from behind; helmet, cloak and sword read clearly.
   **Flat magenta #FF00FF background.**
3. `mon_troll_t1_front` (**strike: smash**): a Norse troll, bigger and bulkier than a hero but the same framing:
   hunched, long drooping nose, two tusks, shaggy grey-green hair and skin, a huge club made of a
   tree root gripped at the thin end, a ragged hide loincloth. Dim and brutal. **Flat magenta
   #FF00FF background.**
4. `mon_troll_t1_back` (**strike: smash**): the same troll from behind; hunched back and club. **Flat magenta #FF00FF
   background.**
5. `mon_harpy_t1_front` (**strike: upward**): a Greek harpy: a gaunt, wild-haired bird-woman with big brown-bronze
   feathered wings instead of arms, sharp yellow talons, a screeching mouth, hovering just above
   the ground (talons at the feet line). Flat green #00FF00 background.
6. `mon_harpy_t1_back` (**strike: upward**): the same harpy from behind; wings spread. Flat green #00FF00 background.
