# G14: hero back capes for the skeleton rig (1 sheet)

Track: heroes. Art request 600 (Stefan, 2026-10-17, decisions 5b287a1: rated the G12 cloaks 2,
"only work on heroes who don't raise their arms"). Replaces `kit_cloaks` from G12.

**Look:** your G12 cloaks redo (same four colour schemes, plain bronze clasps, muted, feathers
only on Odin's and Freyja's), in the one style (`STYLE.md`). **Check against `QA.md`** at full
size (rules 1, 2, 4, 5, 13).

**Drawn for the skeleton:** each cape is a **back layer**: it hangs from the collar line at the
top **straight down the hero's back**, behind the torso and both arms, seen from the front so
only its **edges show either side of the body** and its hem below the knees. **Nothing over the
shoulders or the upper arms**, no sleeves, no hood. The top edge is a **straight collar band**
with the clasp at its centre, so the game pins it at the neck; the cape is **symmetric** left to
right. Each cape about as wide as the G13 torso plus a hand's width each side.

1. `kit_capes`, 2 x 2, flat magenta #FF00FF, one cape per cell, centred, wide margins:
   **`cape_odin`** (dusty blue-grey, pale gold trim, raven feathers at the clasp),
   **`cape_thor`** (dull brick red, gold trim, fur along the collar band),
   **`cape_loki`** (dark moss green, ragged hem), **`cape_freyja`** (faded rose, cream trim,
   falcon feathers at the clasp).
