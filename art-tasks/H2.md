# H2: the eight Aesir paths at tier 2, as parts sheets (8 images)

Track: heroes. Art request 280. **Run after H1 is shipped**: each path keeps its god child's
face, hair and palette from `public/assets/units/hero_<god>_t1_parts.png` (fetch raw).

Follow `STYLE.md` (Stefan's taste test: **big-head cartoon, thin colour-matched outlines,
painterly shading, warm, slightly muted palette**) and `docs/asset-spec.md` **"Parts sheets"**.
Look reference: fetch raw `docs/reference/style-test-1.png` for proportions; follow `STYLE.md`,
not its thick black outlines. Looks come from Design's table, `docs/systems/paths-and-skills.md`
section 6 ("How each path looks").

**Each item is one parts sheet:** a square image split into a **5 x 2 grid**, one part centred in
each cell, nothing crossing into another cell, **flat magenta #FF00FF everywhere else**, all parts
at **the same scale**, facing right. Cells, left to right, top row first: **body** (torso with
clothing) · **head** (face; a beard belongs to the head) · **headgear** (hair, hood or helmet) ·
**cape** · **weapon** (alone, by its grip) / **front foot** · **back foot** · **front hand** ·
**back hand** · **shield** (empty if none). **No effects** in any part (no sparks, flames, glow,
ravens, clouds, shimmer): the game adds them. A path keeps its god child's face, hair and
palette (`docs/asset-spec.md` Tiers).

1. `hero_odin-seer_t2_parts`: tall hat, eye-topped staff; the child of Odin's beard and eye patch.
2. `hero_odin-wanderer_t2_parts`: a spear, a grey travelling cloak, a tunic instead of the robe.
3. `hero_thor-hammerborn_t2_parts`: an iron helm without horns, plate armour, a red and cream round
   shield, the war hammer.
4. `hero_thor-stormcaller_t2_parts`: a lightning staff (iron rod, no lightning drawn), a blue
   cape and tunic.
5. `hero_loki-trickster_t2_parts`: a horned purple hood, pale violet daggers.
6. `hero_loki-flamebinder_t2_parts`: a darker hood with orange trim, empty open hands (the game
   adds the flames), a short wand as weapon.
7. `hero_freyja-valkyrie_t2_parts`: a winged helm, a spear, a blue round shield, light plate.
8. `hero_freyja-seidkona_t2_parts`: a frost hood, a frost staff (pale wood with a blue crystal),
   an ice-blue cape.
