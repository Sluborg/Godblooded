Track: ui. Art request 140 (backlog Art 120 and 130). Draw in the look of `STYLE.md` (Stefan's taste test: thin colour-matched outlines, painterly
shading, warm, slightly muted palette) and match the U8 icons.

Follow `STYLE.md`. Square canvas at your normal output size, one item per image, **flat magenta
#FF00FF background**, no text. Each is a **single bold symbol** filling about **80% of the
canvas**, centred, no plate. These float **small over a hero's or monster's head** in a fight,
so they must **read at 24 px**: one clear shape, one strong colour plus a dark outline.
