# A1: the four Aesir gods at tier 1 and their eight paths at tier 2 (12 images)

Track: heroes. Art request 70 (backlog Art 80). Design: `docs/game-design.md` "Gods and paths".

There are no classes. Every hero is a **Godblood**, a mortal-born child of a god. Tier 1 is one
picture per god (the fresh recruit). At tier 2 the hero takes one of the god's two **paths**: the
same character, clearly upgraded and turned toward that path.

Follow `STYLE.md` (chunky pixel art, faces right, weapon placed by the strike) and
`docs/asset-spec.md`. **Look reference: `art-tasks/ref/chunky-warrior.png`** (fetch it raw): match
its proportions (about 3.5 to 4 heads, big head, big hands and feet, oversized weapon), thick dark
outline, pixel shading and camera. Square canvas at your normal output size, one item per image,
nothing else in it.

Every unit is **one front picture**, no frames and no back view: the game mirrors it and does
all motion in code.

- **front:** faces RIGHT, a three-quarter view toward the lower right.
- Whole figure visible, feet at about 88% of the canvas height, figure about 75% of the canvas
  height, centred, wide planted stance. Nothing cut off, the whole weapon inside the picture.
- **Strike** (table in `STYLE.md`): it fixes where the weapon is drawn, so the game's strike
  starts there. Each item names it and spells out the pose.
- Keep the silhouette compact: no wide effects, spread wings or birds flying away from the body.

**Tier 1 = a fresh recruit:** worn leather, wool and linen, simple iron, one small sign of the
parent god, no glow. **Tier 2 = the same hero after taking a path:** draw it from that god's
tier-1 item in this batch and keep face, hair and build recognisable; better armour and weapon,
the path's colours, one divine motif, a small glow allowed (no big aura; that is tier 3).
