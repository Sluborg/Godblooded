# A1: the healer and the six Aesir tier-2 favor paths (7 images)

Track: heroes. Art request 60. Design: `docs/game-design.md` "Favor paths".

Follow `STYLE.md` (chunky pixel art, faces right, weapon placed by the strike) and
`docs/asset-spec.md`. **Look reference: `art-tasks/ref/chunky-warrior.png`** (fetch it raw): match
its proportions (about 3.5 to 4 heads, big head, big hands and feet, oversized weapon), thick dark
outline, pixel shading and camera. Square canvas at your normal output size, one item per image,
nothing else in it.

Every unit is **one front picture**, no frames and no back view: the game mirrors it and does
all motion in code.

- **front:** faces RIGHT, a three-quarter view toward the lower right.
- Whole figure visible, feet at about 88% of the canvas height, figure about 75% of the canvas
  height, centred, wide planted stance. Nothing cut off, the whole weapon inside the picture.
- **Strike** (table in `STYLE.md`): it fixes where the weapon is drawn, so the game's strike
  starts there. Each item names it and spells out the pose.
- Keep the silhouette compact: no wide effects, wings or birds flying away from the body.

**Tier 2 = the tier-1 hero after the god chose them:** the same character, clearly upgraded:
better armour and weapon, the god's accent colour, one divine motif, a small glow allowed (no big
aura; that is tier 3). Each path starts from its shipped tier-1 class; fetch it raw from
`public/assets/units/hero_<class>_t1_front.png` and keep face, hair and build recognisable.

1. `hero_healer_t1_front` (**strike: bolt**): a young Godblood healer, the new sixth class, tier 1
   (fresh recruit: worn linen and leather, no glow). **Class colour: pale gold and off-white**
   robe and hood, a satchel of herbs and bandages, a small clay flask on the belt. **Pose:** a
   short wooden staff with a small hanging charm held in the **FRONT hand**, its head raised at
   shoulder height in front of the body; the other hand open. Kind but tired face. **Flat magenta
   #FF00FF background.**
2. `hero_warrior-thor_t2_front` (**strike: overhead chop**): **Storm Berserker**, the warrior
   (`hero_warrior_t1_front`) chosen by Thor, like the Daughter of Thor in
   `art-tasks/ref/concept-thor-t1-t2.png`: heavier iron and leather, a wolf-fur mantle, storm
   blue-white accents, a small crackle of lightning on the weapon. **Pose:** a big bearded axe
   (or Thor-style hammer) **raised high behind the back shoulder**, gripped at the lower end.
   **Flat magenta #FF00FF background.**
3. `hero_paladin-tyr_t2_front` (**strike: overhead chop**): **Oathsworn**, the paladin
   (`hero_paladin_t1_front`) chosen by Tyr, god of law and oaths: polished iron mail, a plain
   iron helm, a tabard with Tyr's rune (an upward arrow), a **big round shield** on the front arm.
   **Pose:** a one-handed sword **raised high behind the back shoulder**. Stern, honourable.
   **Flat green #00FF00 background.**
4. `hero_ranger-heimdall_t2_front` (**strike: shot**): **Bifrost Warden**, the ranger
   (`hero_ranger_t1_front`) chosen by Heimdall, the watchman of the gods: a better longbow, a
   bronze-gold horn (the Gjallarhorn) on the belt, a hooded cloak with a thin trim in blue, gold
   and red (rainbow bridge accents, no green or pink). **Pose:** the bow held **in front of the
   body at chest height**, an arrow nocked, pointing right. **Flat magenta #FF00FF background.**
5. `hero_rogue-loki_t2_front` (**strike: double chop**): **Rune Trickster**, the rogue
   (`hero_rogue_t1_front`) chosen by Loki, like the Son of Loki in
   `art-tasks/ref/concept-loki-t2.png`: tattered dark green hooded cloak, gold trim, a carved
   trickster mask hanging at the belt, faint green rune glow. **Pose:** two daggers, **both
   clearly visible**: one raised high behind the back shoulder, one held low in front of the
   body. **Flat magenta #FF00FF background.**
6. `hero_wizard-odin_t2_front` (**strike: bolt**): **Raven Seer**, the wizard
   (`hero_wizard_t1_front`) chosen by Odin: a dark navy cloak and wide-brimmed hat, a patch over
   one eye, glowing blue runes on the robe, **one raven perched on the shoulder**. **Pose:** a
   tall spear-staff held in the **FRONT hand**, its spearhead raised at shoulder height in front
   of the body; a faint rune glow in the free hand. **Flat magenta #FF00FF background.**
7. `hero_healer-freyja_t2_front` (**strike: bolt**): **Valkyrie Skald**, the healer (item 1)
   chosen by Freyja, like the Son of Freyja in `art-tasks/ref/concept-freyja-t2.png`: a white fur
   mantle, a feathered cloak in deep blue and violet, gold trim, braided blond hair. **Pose:** a
   small golden harp held in the **FRONT hand** at shoulder height in front of the body, strings
   faintly glowing (the harp is the focus). **Flat green #00FF00 background.**
