# G8: title key art redo: crisp, readable monsters, a mixed party (1 image)

Track: ui. Art request 480 (Stefan's rating of G5, 2026-10-15, rated 2: "So grainy you can
hardly see the monsters. All heroes have the exact same stance. Why not show more than Aesir?
Parties can probably be mixed. But graininess is the biggest issue."). Replaces `art_splash`.
The title screen shows it full screen with the logo and buttons on top in code: **no text and
no logo** in the picture.

Follow `STYLE.md` (one style; read the new rules **"No grain"** and **"Groups vary"**) and
`docs/asset-spec.md`. Fetch raw the hero anchor `art-tasks/ref/hero-anchor-grim.png`, the
buildings `public/assets/buildings/bld_townhall_t1.png` and `bld_tower_t1.png`, and the monsters
`public/assets/units/mon_<type>_t1_front.png`, and paint them the same way. **Check against
`QA.md`** at full size before uploading (rule 13 is the reason for this batch).

**Crisp, no grain (most important):** paint like the shipped buildings: **clean, flat pixel
clusters, large calm areas of one colour**. No noise texture, no film grain, no speckle, no
dither pattern over the ground or the sky, no blur, no haze. Fewer, bigger shapes. Zoom to 100%
before upload: if any area looks like static or sand, redo it smoother.

**Canvas and framing:** landscape **1536 x 1024**, painted edge to edge (no key colour). Keep
everything important between **12% and 88% of the height** (the game crops it to 16:9). Keep the
**upper middle calm** (sky, a low sun: the logo goes there) and the **lower middle calm** (open
ground: the buttons go there).
