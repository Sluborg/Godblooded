# G13: rig-ready hero body: torso, arms and legs as separate pieces (5 sheets)

**Draft, do not start.** Track: heroes. Art request 570 (Stefan, 2026-10-17: heroes move to a
real skeleton, bones and IK: shoulder, upper arm, forearm, hand; hip, thigh, shin, foot). The
G12 armour has sleeves painted on the torso, and cutting them off leaves sleeve tips behind, so
the body is redrawn as **one piece per bone**. The proportions are still being tested by
Stefan: the sizes below are placeholders until Lead sends the final ones. Art ships the pieces
as `kit_` rows after Stefan's rating.

**Look:** exactly your approved **G7** heroes and the G12 sheets (fetch `art-tasks/G7.md` and
`art-tasks/G12.md` raw from main): painted pixel art in the one style (`STYLE.md`), worn real
gear, thin dark-brown outline, light from the top left, muted earthy colours. **Check every
sheet against `QA.md`** at full size (rules 1, 2, 4, 5, 13).

**Every sheet:** square canvas at your normal output size, **flat magenta #FF00FF**, a 3 x 2 grid
of equal cells, **one piece per cell, centred, with wide clear magenta around it**, nothing
touching a cell edge or another piece. Art scales every piece itself, so draw each one large;
keep the pieces of one sheet at the same scale as each other.

**Drawn for a skeleton** (the game bends every joint, so every piece must survive rotation):

- **Front view, hanging straight down.** Every arm and leg piece points straight down, seen
  from the front, no bend, no foreshortening, no hand or foot attached.
- **Round, closed joint ends.** The top of every limb piece (shoulder, elbow, hip, knee) is a
  **rounded cap** that is painted all the way round, so it still looks whole when the piece
  turns under the piece above it. No cut edge, no seam, no stray tips of another piece.
- **Overlap room.** Draw each limb piece a little longer at its top end (about a sixth of its
  length) so it can tuck under the piece above it.
- **Torso without arms:** shoulders **rounded and closed** like a vest, no sleeves, no sleeve
  tips, an open **neck opening** at the top (the head's collar tucks in), the hem or skirt at
  the bottom, no legs.
- **One of each limb:** draw one arm and one leg; the game draws the far one from the same
  piece.
- Art marks the pivots (shoulder, elbow, wrist, hip, knee, ankle) itself; do **not** paint dots
  or marks.
