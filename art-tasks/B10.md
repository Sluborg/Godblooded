# B10: monster lairs tier 1, all six (6 images)

Track: buildings. Art request 110 (Lead 2026-10-13: lairs first, for Sim 150). Lairs sit out in the fog on the map and spawn
their monsters; heroes hunt them down. Each lair must read at a glance as **whose home it is**.
Tier 2 and 3 (the lair grows) come in a later batch, so keep the tier-1 lair small and humble.

Follow `STYLE.md` (chunky pixel art: crisp pixels, thick dark outline, gritty palette) and
`docs/asset-spec.md`. Look reference for the rendering: `art-tasks/ref/chunky-warrior.png` (fetch
it raw): same outline weight, pixel shading and light from the top left. The monsters themselves
are in `public/assets/units/mon_<type>_t1_front.png` (fetch raw) for colours and mood. Square
canvas at your normal output size, one item per image, nothing else in it.

- 3/4 top-down game camera seen from the lower right, the same camera as the town buildings.
- Whole lair visible, its base centred, about 85% of the canvas width. Chunky and a little
  oversized in its details so it reads at phone size.
- No monster in the picture, no ground around it beyond the lair's own footprint, no shadow.
- Darker and wilder than the town: bones, moss, rot, smoke. Tier 1 = small and crude.

1. `bld_lair_barrow_t1`: **draugr barrow**: a low grassy burial mound with a stone-lined
   entrance, two leaning standing stones with faded runes, a rusty sword stuck in the earth, a
   cold blue-green glow in the doorway. **Flat magenta #FF00FF background.**
2. `bld_lair_troll-den_t1`: **troll den**: a cave mouth in a heap of big grey boulders, gnawed
   bones and a broken cart wheel in front, a crude wooden fence of sharpened stakes, smoke from a
   crack in the rock. **Flat magenta #FF00FF background.**
3. `bld_lair_harpy-roost_t1`: **harpy roost**: a jagged rock spire with a huge nest of sticks and
   torn cloth on top, feathers and bones scattered at its foot, a few stolen shiny trinkets in the
   nest. **Flat magenta #FF00FF background.**
4. `bld_lair_labyrinth_t1`: **minotaur labyrinth**: the ruined entrance of a stone maze: a
   broken Greek-style gateway with a carved bull head on the lintel, low walls running off into
   the dark, bull horns on a post, scattered bones. **Flat magenta #FF00FF background.**
5. `bld_lair_jackal-camp_t1`: **jackal-man camp**: two ragged hide tents on sticks, a smoking
   campfire ring, a crude totem pole topped with a jackal skull, a broken Egyptian pot and a
   pile of loot. **Flat magenta #FF00FF background.**
6. `bld_lair_wyrm-nest_t1` (the **boss lair**, a little bigger and grander than the others):
   **wyrm nest**: a scorched hollow among black rocks, a ring of huge cracked eggshells, a hoard
   of gold coins and broken weapons, smoke and a few embers rising. **Flat magenta #FF00FF
   background.**
