   building footprint. **Flat magenta #FF00FF background.**
3. `bld_scaffold`: a **timber scaffold** that the game lays **over** a building while it is

   solid behind them, so the building shows through. About **85% of the canvas width**.
   **Flat magenta #FF00FF background.**
4. `ter_grave_aesir`: an Aesir grave marker for a fallen hero: a small upright **rune stone**

   bottom. **Flat magenta #FF00FF background.**
5. `ter_grave_greek`: a Greek grave marker: a small marble **stele** with a carved laurel and a
