# G10: monster look test on the compact rig: minotaur (2 images)

Track: monsters. Art request 520 (Stefan, 2026-10-15 evening: "Make RimWorld minotaur instead";
Lead confirmed: the compact rig, the same as the heroes). A look test, nothing ships.

The compact rig is the hero look of `G7.md` (fetch it raw from main and read it): a **big head,
a short solid body, small separate hands and hooves that float** beside and under the body (no
arms, no legs drawn), painted in the one style (`STYLE.md`), grim and believable gear. Fetch raw
`docs/reference/style-test-1.png` for the proportions only. The game moves hands and hooves in
code. A monster is **heavier than a hero**: broader body, bigger head, about **2.5 heads tall**,
the same scale as a hero (it stands about one head taller than Grim).

Square canvas, **flat magenta #FF00FF background**. **Check against `QA.md`** at full size
(rules 1, 3, 10, 11; it must read at 40 px).

1. `g10_minotaur`: the **assembled** minotaur, front view with a slight three-quarter turn to
   the right, figure about 75% of the canvas height, feet at about 88%: a huge bull head with
   curved horns and a nose ring, a short broad furred body with a leather harness and bronze
   belt, two big floating fists, two floating cloven hooves, a **double axe with a straight haft**
   held in the front fist, pointing up. Grim and heavy, not cute.
2. `kit_minotaur`: **the same minotaur as separate parts** on one sheet, a 3 x 2 grid, each part
   centred in its cell with space around it, nothing touching: row 1 **head** (with horns), **body**
   (torso with harness and belt, no head, no limbs), **hand** (one fist; its grip is redrawn in `G11.md` item 5, no hole);
   row 2 **hoof** (one), **double axe** (pointing up, straight haft), an empty cell. Same colours,
   outline and light as item 1.
