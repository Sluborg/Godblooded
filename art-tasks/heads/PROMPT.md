# Heads track: prompt rules

Use the template as the image reference for every head:
`https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Sluborg/Godblooded/main/art-tasks/heads/head-template.png?t=<current time>`.
It is two squares side by side. The **left square** is the guide; the **right square** is an
example head in the same framing. Your picture is **one square like the left one**.

## Canvas and placement

- **1024 x 1024 px**, background **flat magenta #FF00FF** (no gradient, no shadow, no texture).
- The head **fills the grey skull shape** of the guide, at that size and in that place.
- **Eyes on the red line.**
- **The face centre line** (nose, mouth, chin) **on the blue line.**
- **The ear on the orange dot.**
- **The chin on the green dot.**
- **Hair only inside the upper dashed box; a beard only inside the lower dashed box.** Nothing
  outside the skull shape and the two boxes.
- **No neck, no throat, no shoulders, no collar, no body.** The head ends at the jaw and the
  beard or chin.
- **3/4 view facing right**, like the example. Copy the example's **angle, size and place only**,
  never its style, face or colours.
- None of the guide marks (grey shape, lines, dots, boxes, text) may appear in your picture.

## Style

- **Adult, grim, stylized chibi:** a big head with simple, readable shapes, stern brows, small dark
  eyes. Grim and adult, never cute, never baby-faced.
- **Painted with soft shading:** light from the top left, a **thin dark outline** around the head
  and darker lines between hair, face and beard.
- **Muted colours:** earthy and natural (umber, ochre, rust, slate, bone). No neon, no pure colours.
- **It must read at 15 px wide:** big masses of hair and beard, a clear silhouette, few details. No
  single hairs, no fine wrinkles, no noise. Shrink it to 15 px in your head: if the face or the
  character is lost, simplify.
- **Women are clearly feminine** (softer jaw, fuller lips, longer lashes or brows shaped
  differently) and still grim warriors.

## Check (every head, before delivering)

1. Square 1024 x 1024, flat #FF00FF everywhere outside the head, no marks or text.
2. Eyes on the red line, face centre on the blue line, ear on the orange dot, chin on the green dot.
3. Hair and beard inside their boxes; no neck, shoulders or body.
4. 3/4 facing right, the same size as the example.
5. Shrunk to 15 px wide it still reads as this character.
