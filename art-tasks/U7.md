# U7: omen icons (8 images)

Track: ui. Art request 160 (backlog Art 160). Draw in the **new look** (`STYLE.md`: cartoon shapes, thick dark outline, painterly finish). Design:
`docs/systems/events.md` ("Omens"). An omen is a divine event that lasts about a minute; its
icon shows in the omen banner and at the top of the screen.

Follow `STYLE.md`. Square canvas at your normal output size, one item per image, **flat magenta
#FF00FF background**, no text. Each a **single bold symbol** filling about **80% of the
canvas**, centred, no plate, **read at 32 px**. Omens may glow a little (they are divine).

1. `ui_omen_fallenstar`: a falling star with a short burning tail, gold and white.
2. `ui_omen_champion`: a horned helmet over two crossed swords, dark red (a wandering champion).
3. `ui_omen_caravan`: a covered wagon wheel with a coin purse, bronze and cream.
4. `ui_omen_redsky`: a blood-red sun low over a jagged black horizon.
5. `ui_omen_fimbulfrost`: a frozen wolf's head with icicles, frost blue (Aesir).
6. `ui_omen_olympia`: an olive wreath around a small bronze discus (Greek).
7. `ui_omen_scarabs`: a gold-and-black scarab beetle with its wings open (Egyptian).
8. `ui_omen_eclipse`: a black sun disc with a gold corona ring, Ra's falcon outline (Egyptian).
