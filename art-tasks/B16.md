# B16: buildings tier 2 in the new look, all nine (9 images)

Track: buildings. Art request 240 (Lead 2026-10-13). **Run it only after B15 and B18 are shipped**
(both Done in `NEXT.md`; the town hall's tier 1 comes from B18): each item is the upgrade of the **new** tier-1 picture.

Follow `STYLE.md` (Stefan's taste test: **big-head cartoon, thin colour-matched outlines,
painterly shading, warm, slightly muted palette**) and `docs/asset-spec.md`. Look reference: fetch
raw `docs/reference/style-test-1.png` and `docs/reference/style-test-2.png` for the camera, the
building shapes and how small a hero is next to a building; follow `STYLE.md`, not their thick
black outlines. **Each item is the upgrade of its tier-1 picture**: fetch it raw from
`public/assets/buildings/bld_<type>_t1.png` and keep its layout, main shape and colours
recognisable. Square canvas at your normal output size, one item per image, nothing else in it.

- 3/4 top-down game camera seen from the lower right, the same camera for every building.
- Whole building visible, its base centred, about **90% of the canvas width**, a clear margin
  on every side. Chunky, slightly oversized details (doors, beams, roof ornaments) so it reads at
  phone size. **Scale:** a hero is small next to it (the town hall is about 5 heroes wide).
- No ground, grass, path or scenery around it, no shadow.
- **Tier 2 = proven and prosperous:** the same building rebuilt bigger and sturdier: dressed
  stone footing, solid timber, iron and bronze fittings, fresh paint in its accent colour,
  banners or crests. The silhouette must change (a storey, a wing, a tower or a porch), not only
  the colours. At most a hint of an effect (a lit window, a faintly glowing rune);
  the clear glow comes at tier 3.

1. `bld_townhall_t2`: the B18 great hall grown into a jarl's hall: a side wing with its own
   roof ridge, bigger carved dragon heads, the plinth and steps in dressed stone, iron-studded
   double doors, a small watch platform on the roof; keep its banners and braziers. **Flat magenta #FF00FF background.**
2. `bld_market_t2`: a covered market hall: a timber roof over four stalls, the red-and-cream
   striped awnings fresh, crates of apples, bread, cloth bolts and spices, barrels, a hanging
   scale and a carved signboard. **Flat magenta #FF00FF background.**
3. `bld_smithy_t2`: a bigger forge: a stone workshop with two chimneys, a large bellows, a
   glowing hearth, two anvils, a quench barrel and a full rack of axes, swords, shields and
   helmets outside. **Flat magenta #FF00FF background.**
4. `bld_shrine_t2`: a larger healer's shrine: a round stone sanctuary with a cloister porch on
   wooden posts, a wide stone basin with a small spring spout, rows of lit candles, hanging
   herbs and white linen banners. **Flat magenta #FF00FF background.**
5. `bld_tower_t2`: a stone watchtower: square, taller than tier 1, a battlemented lookout with a
   pointed timber roof, arrow slits, a mounted crossbow on the platform, a red banner and the
   horn by the rail. **Flat magenta #FF00FF background.**
6. `bld_temple_aesir_t2`: a full Norse stave church: three stacked steep shingle roofs with
   carved dragon heads, a carved portal, two rune stones by the door, frost-blue and silver
   painted trim, a small bell or horn post. **Flat magenta #FF00FF background.**
7. `bld_temple_greek_t2`: a larger marble temple: six columns, a carved pediment relief, a wide
   stepped base, two bronze braziers, bronze-gold trim and a small statue on the roof ridge.
   **Flat magenta #FF00FF background.**
8. `bld_temple_egyptian_t2`: a grand sandstone pylon temple: taller sloped towers with flag
   poles and pennants, two tall obelisks, a pair of small sphinx statues at the door, bright
   lapis-blue and gold painted bands. **Flat magenta #FF00FF background.**
9. `bld_hearth-hall_t2`: the Hearth Hall rebuilt: a second hearth with its own chimney, round
   shields hung along the walls, a carved door. **Flat magenta #FF00FF background.**
