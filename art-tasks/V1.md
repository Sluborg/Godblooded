# V1: monster variants, two per common monster (8 images)

Track: monsters. Art request 40. A pack of four draugr must not look cloned, so each common
monster gets two variants.

**Every item is an EDIT of the shipped base picture, not a new drawing.** Fetch the base raw from
the repo (`assets/source/B4/<id>.png` or `assets/source/B5/<id>.png`, the untouched upload) and
edit it: **same canvas size, same pose, same body, same position on the canvas**. Change only:

- the **head**: helmet, hair, horns, face markings, as the item says;
- the **weapon** (or claws): a different weapon of the same size, **held in exactly the same
  place and angle**, so the strike still starts at the same point.

Keep the colours of the body and clothing, the outline weight and the shading. Nothing else in the
picture, **flat magenta #FF00FF background** as in the base.

1. `mon_draugr-v2_t1_front` (**strike: overhead chop**), base `assets/source/B4/mon_draugr_t1_front.png`:
   a horned iron helmet with cheek guards over the skull face; the sword becomes a **bearded
   war axe**, raised high behind the back shoulder in the same place.
2. `mon_draugr-v3_t1_front` (**strike: overhead chop**), same base: a bare skull with long
   white braids and a crown of rusted iron spikes; the sword becomes a **broken-tipped longsword**
   with a bone hilt, same place.
3. `mon_harpy-v2_t1_front` (**strike: upward**), base `assets/source/B4/mon_harpy_t1_front.png`:
   raven-black hair with a crest of black feathers and a bone mask over the eyes; the talons
   become **black obsidian claws**, same place.
4. `mon_harpy-v3_t1_front` (**strike: upward**), same base: ash-grey hair, pale owl-like face
   markings around the eyes; the talons become **blood-red claws**, same place.
5. `mon_jackal-man-v2_t1_front` (**strike: side sweep**), base
   `assets/source/B5/mon_jackal-man_t1_front.png`: a striped gold and lapis nemes headdress; the
   khopesh becomes a **curved bronze war axe**, held level behind the hip in the same place.
6. `mon_jackal-man-v3_t1_front` (**strike: side sweep**), same base: grey-furred scarred head with
   one torn ear and a bone earring; the khopesh becomes a **long curved sickle-sword**, same
   place.
7. `mon_minotaur-v2_t1_front` (**strike: overhead chop**), base
   `assets/source/B5/mon_minotaur_t1_front.png`: black-furred head with one broken horn and an
   iron nose ring; the double axe becomes a **single-bladed great axe**, raised high behind the
   back shoulder in the same place.
8. `mon_minotaur-v3_t1_front` (**strike: overhead chop**), same base: white bull head with
   gold-capped horns; the double axe becomes a **big stone maul**, same place.
