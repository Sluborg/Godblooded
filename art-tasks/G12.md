# G12: the hero kit from G7, first batch (11 sheets, 41 parts)

Track: heroes. Art request 560 (Stefan, 2026-10-16: G7 approved as the hero look, rated 5; "the
hero kit from G7 as separate parts"). Part list: Design (`docs/systems/hero-kit.md` section 5,
first batch). Sockets and sizes: Scene (the compact floating rig). Art ships the sheets as `kit_`
rows after Stefan's rating.

**Look:** exactly your approved **G7** heroes (fetch `art-tasks/G7.md` raw from main; you made
them on 2026-10-15): painted pixel art in the one style (`STYLE.md`), grim adult faces, real worn
gear, thin dark-brown outline, light from the top left. **Check every sheet against `QA.md`** at
full size (rules 1, 3, 4, 11 and the shield rule).

**Every sheet:** square canvas at your normal output size, **flat magenta #FF00FF**, a grid of
equal cells (the item says which), **one part per cell, centred, with clear magenta around it,
nothing touching a cell edge or another part**. All parts face **right**, front view with a slight
three-quarter turn, the way they sit on a G7 hero. **No hands or arms on anything** except the
`hand` parts; no bodies under heads, no heads on torsos. Keep **one scale on every sheet**: the
sizes below are in units of a 44-unit hero (a G7 head is 19 units wide), so a head and a torso are
about the same width, a hand about a third of a head.

1. `kit_base`, 2 x 2: **`torso_base`** (a plain belted wool tunic, the base under all armour),

   grim, no headgear on any head (hair shows).
